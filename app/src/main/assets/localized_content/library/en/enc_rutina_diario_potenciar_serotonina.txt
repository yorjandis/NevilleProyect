Practical, realistic and sustainable daily routine to optimize serotonin using simple habits that act on the brain, intestine and circadian rhythm. It is not “extreme biohacking”, but well-applied physiology.

🟢 Morning (activate serotonin and circadian rhythm)

🔸Early sunlight (10–30 min)

What to do
    • Go outside as soon as you wake up
    • No sunglasses if possible
    • Walk or simply expose yourself to light

Why it works
    • Sunlight increases serotonin synthesis in the brain
    • Synchronizes the biological clock → better mood during the day and better melatonin at night

Example:
    • Walk 15 minutes after getting up


🔸Gentle movement (10–20 min)

Options
    • Walk
    • Joint mobility
    • Light yoga
    • Stretching

Why it works
    • Exercise stimulates the release of serotonin and reduces cortisol
    • Improves the gut-brain signal

Example:
    • Walk + basic mobility before sitting down to work


🔸Pro-serotonin breakfast

Objective
Provide tryptophan + complex carbohydrates + healthy fats

Practical examples
    • Natural yogurt or kefir + oats + nuts + banana
    • Eggs + whole wheat bread + avocado
    • Oatmeal cooked with seeds and red fruits

🚫 Avoid:
    • Sugars alone (serotonin peaks → dips)
    • Ultra processed breakfasts



🟢 Mid-morning (emotional stability)

🔸Regulation pause (5 min)

What to do
    • Slow nasal breathing
    • Rhythm: 4–5 s inhale / 6–7 s exhale

Why it works
    • Reduces cortisol (the silent enemy of serotonin)
    • Improves parasympathetic signal

Quick tip:
Do it before coffee or demanding meetings.


🟢 Noon (happy gut = stable brain)

🔸Balanced food

ideal structure
    • Protein → tryptophan
    • Complex carbohydrate
    • Fiber + vegetables
    • Somewhat fermented

Examples
    • Lentils + brown rice + vegetables
    • Fish + potato or quinoa + salad
    • Chicken or tofu + legumes + vegetables

Add:
    • Natural yogurt
    • Sauerkraut
    • Kefir



🔸Walk 10-15 minutes after eating

why
    • Improves digestion
    • Reduces inflammation
    • Optimizes intestinal serotonin production


🟢 Late (maintain stable levels)

🔸Main exercise (30–45 min)

Best options
    • Strength training
    • Run easy
    • Bicycle
    • Swimming

Ideal frequency:
    • 4–5 days per week

Why it works
    • Increases serotonin and serotonergic receptors
    • Reduces basal anxiety



🔸Social contact or purpose

Options
    • Real conversation (not just chat)
    • Help someone
    • Creative activity
    • Meaningful work

Example:
    • Call a friend
    • Teach someone something
    • Advance in a personal project

➡️ This raises serotonin deeply and sustainably (not fast dopamine).



🟢 Night (consolidate serotonin → melatonin)

🔸Light and early dinner

Examples
    • Vegetables + protein
    • Soup + egg or fish
    • Yogurt/kefir with seeds

🚫 Avoid:
    • Heavy meals
    • Alcohol
    • Night sugar


🔸Digital hygiene (60–90 min before bed)

What to do
    • Dim lights
    • Avoid bright screens
    • Read, write or stretch

why
    • Allows serotonin to be transformed into melatonin
    • Improves sleep quality and mood the next day



🔸Closing ritual (5–10 min)

Options
    • Gratitude (3 things of the day)
    • Slow breathing
    • Short writing

📌 Example:
“Today I did something useful, something I enjoyed and something that calmed me down.”



🟢 Important keys (so as not to sabotage serotonin)

Avoid excess:
    • Sleep little
    • Frequent alcohol
    • Ultra-processed
    • Chronic stress without breaks
    • Social isolation


🟢 Ultra practical summary

If you only did 5 things:
    1. Sunlight in the morning
    2. Daily movement
    3. Eat tryptophan + fiber
    4. Sleep well
    5. Consciously reduce stress

👉 Serotonin regulates itself.
