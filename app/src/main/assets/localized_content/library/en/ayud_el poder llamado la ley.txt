I ask you to use this power called The Law. Simply determine what you want and imagine a scene that implies you have accomplished it. Enter the spirit of the scene, participate in it, giving it sensory vividness. Then, relax as you feel its reality. Do not consider the means; be aware that your desire is already a done deal and that you are enjoying it now. Then, have faith, for faith is loyalty to your invisible reality. Your imagined act, though invisible, is a reality because God made it so.

If I were to ask you, "Who is imagining it?" you would answer, "I am," and that is the name of God, forever and ever! Learn to live in your imagination morning, noon, and night.
