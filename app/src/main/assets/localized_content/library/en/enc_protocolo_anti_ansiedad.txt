Effective Daily Protocol against Anxiety

Daily anti-anxiety protocol based on neuroscience, serotonin regulation and nervous system, designed to be realistic, sustainable and effective. It does not seek to “eliminate” anxiety by force, but rather to deactivate the state of threat that maintains it.

Think of it as repeated safety signals to your brain.


🟠 KEY PRINCIPLE

Anxiety is not reasoned, it is regulated.
First calm the body → then the mind.



🟢 Morning (07:00–10:00)

Objective: establish a sense of control and predictability.

🔸Wake up without stimuli (10–20 min)

❌ Mobile
❌ News
❌ Networks

Avoid activating the amygdala and dopamine early.


🔸Sunlight + gentle movement (20–30 min)

Options:
    • Walk
    • Stretching
    • Joint mobility

Effect:
    • ↑ serotonin
    • ↓ cortisol

Example:

Walk 20 minutes outdoors without headphones.


🔸Regulatory breathing (5 min)

Pattern:
    • Inhale 4 s
    • Exhale 6–8 s (key)

This:
    • Activates vagus nerve
    • Reduces hypervigilance


🔸Fixed boot routine

Always do the same:
    • Same breakfast
    • Same order
    • Same rhythm

Repetition tells the brain:

“The environment is predictable → there is no danger.”


🟢 Noon (12:00–16:00)

Objective: energetic and emotional stability.


🔸Regulatory feeding

Prioritize:
    • Protein
    • Complex carbohydrates
    • Healthy fats

Avoid:
    • Skip meals
    • Sugar spikes
    • Excess caffeine


🔸Parasympathetic movement (10–20 min)

No intense exercise.

Better:
    • Walk
    • Stretching
    • Conscious breathing


🔸Decision reduction

Anxiety increases with:
    • Too many options
    • Constant uncertainty

Application:
    • Simple plan
    • 3 tasks maximum


🟢 Afternoon (17:00–20:00)

Objective: release accumulated tension.


🔸Social contact or nature

Choose at least one:
    • Calm conversation
    • Outdoor walk
    • Shared activity without screens

🧠 Elevates serotonin and oxytocin.


🔸Limited digital window

Rule:
    • Max. 30–45 min
    • No alarming content

Avoid:
    • News
    • Social comparison


🟢 Night (20:00–23:00)

Objective: turn off hypervigilance.


🔸Early and light dinner

Avoid:
    • Alcohol
    • Sugar
    • Stimulants


🔸Closing ritual (very important)

Repeat every night:
    • Warm light
    • Calm activity
    • Same order

Examples:
    • Read
    • Stretch
    • Write 3 lines

The brain learns:

“This moment is safe.”


🔸Deep breathing + body

Simple exercise:
    • 5 minutes slow breathing
    • Focus on physical sensations

This cuts off the rumination.



🔸Screens off (90 min before)

Blue light:
    • Reduces serotonin
    • Sleep worsens
    • Increases nighttime anxiety



🟢 Mini-tool for anxiety attacks (in the moment)

3-3-6 technique
    • Inhale 3 s
    • Hold 3 s
    • Exhale 6 s
× 5 cycles

Then:
    • Name 5 things you see
    • 3 that you touch
    • 1 you hear

This returns to the present.



🟢 Signs that the protocol works

✔ Less basal tension
✔ Quieter mind
✔ Deeper sleep
✔ Less need for control
✔ Shorter and more manageable anxiety


🟢 Common mistakes

❌ Try to “think positive”
❌ Force yourself to perform the same
❌ Seek constant distraction
❌ Consume stimulants to calm yourself



🟢 Key phrase to integrate

You don't need to be on alert all the time.
Your nervous system can learn to rest.



🟢 Important (honestly)

If anxiety:
    • It is persistent
    • Interferes with your life
    • Produces panic attacks
    • Accompanies depression

👉 Professional help is part of the protocol, not an exception.


🟢 Final idea
Anxiety disappears when the body feels safe long enough
