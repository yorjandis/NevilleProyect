Method of Manifestation

If you wish to transcend this level, you must definitively relinquish everything related to your current self-concept. To do this, shift your attention away from all your present problems and limitations and immerse yourself in the CONSCIOUSNESS OF BEING. That is, say to yourself silently but with deep feeling, "I AM." Let this state of consciousness be completely unconditioned. Declare that you ARE, and continue to do so, until you are completely lost in the sensation of simply BEING, without face, without form, without conditioning or relationships.

When you reach this state of consciousness, then, within this formless depth that you now possess, give form to the new conception that you wish to attract into your life, FEELING that it IS what you desire.

You will find that, within this formless depth of yourself, all things are divinely possible. Anything in the world that you can imagine, you can achieve or obtain, within this formless and unconditioned consciousness, and in the most natural way.

Unless you completely abandon your current concept of yourself and embrace the nature of the new desire, you will continue to manifest your limitations and shortcomings in your reality.
