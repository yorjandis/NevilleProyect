How do we avoid being subconsciously influenced?

Consciousness, or the Whole, can be divided into two parts: the subconscious and the conscious. The subconscious is that part of Consciousness that doesn't reason or exercise the power of imagination, but accepts as truth any premise given by the conscious mind and proceeds to externalize it. The conscious is the part of Consciousness that responds to the stimuli of the physical senses and objective faculties. Now, the most important thing here is to recognize that the subconscious accepts any belief that the conscious mind has charged with feeling.

Feeling is what moves the subconscious to action. Therefore, regardless of whether a premise is false or true, if it is charged with feeling, it will be accepted and externalized by the subconscious. Ideas are transmitted to the subconscious through feeling. Furthermore, assumptions in the subconscious are transmitted between minds through telepathy. If an assumption about another person is imbued with feeling, it will reach their subconscious, and if it forms part of their beliefs, then it will influence their behavior.

However, if the transmitted premise (assumption) is not part of their belief system, then it will have no effect and will return to its source, exerting its influence there. In fact, every assumption we have about others will first affect us, since feeling it will register it in our subconscious (which will then externalize it), and then it will reach the recipient's subconscious. In conclusion, every subconscious assumption we send or receive from others only affects us if our beliefs align with the assumption.
