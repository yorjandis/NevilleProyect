"Habit can be our best teacher. I'm going to reveal a truth that you have surely overlooked because of its own power. It is said that Satan is the doubt in our hearts, the inability to sustain an assumption. But I tell you, my friends, doubt or Satan is not the direct cause. It is said in the scriptures that Satan is the father of lies. Isn't an assumption a lie to our senses and reason? But we know it is a truth within the realm of imagination. Therefore, God is everything, black and white. \n\nWhat prevents us from being boldly daring? What spoils our desires and impulses to the point of completely diverting us from our goal? HABIT and its children: Custom, impatience, resignation, doubt. We are slaves to our habits. We want change in our world, but we resign ourselves to our protected space. We are panicked or lazy about entering the new state, and although we know the truth of the power of imagination, we prefer to remain slaves to our habits. How do we overcome habit and break free from our current, unwanted state? We struggle to accept and apply truths under the influence of this enemy master. The Power of Love is the greatest power we can experience. Love is capable of instantly lifting us out of any state. How? When you love something, you become that thing. Love your desire! Be passionate about the new state, forget yourself, forget the world, forget what others think. Only what you love matters. When you come to love your new state, Love will snatch you from Satan's grasp. And you will learn to love even the father of lies. For God is everything, both black and white."
