"The physical world is only energy; our senses and reason interpret it according to our preconceived ideas and judgments. Something becomes true when we identify with it; then we allow it to express itself in our world. On a physical-emotional level, we are all separate because of our individualized subjectivity. However, at the level of our common origin, we are all one consciousness, a great creative and expansive force that we call the human imagination. We are powerful creators, loving, individualized flames who, like children, play at feeling incapable and small. We are frightened by the shadows of our dreams and laugh at our own enchantments. What is life if not the expression and expansion of this loving being within each of us? The being that we truly are!"
