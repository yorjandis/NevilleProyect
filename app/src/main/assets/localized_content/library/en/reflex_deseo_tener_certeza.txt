"contenido": [
"To desire something is to acknowledge that it is not currently present in your experience. Desire creates the necessary void for consciousness to fill with your Assumption. But we must not remain in the desire, because we will only perpetuate that void. Once the desire is identified, we must quickly move to feeling and living as if the desired thing were already a reality. The void created by the desire must be filled with the Assumption of its fulfillment. Finally, having conversations and living our Assumption will bring the \"physical filling\" to this dimension. Remember, desire is the Father's intention to manifest. But we must provoke the movement of energy by bringing the desired state into our immediate present. Everything comes first from within and then imprints its mark on our sensory reality."
