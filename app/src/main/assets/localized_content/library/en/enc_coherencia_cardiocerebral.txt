Cardiac and brain coherence: the connection between mind and heart

From neuroscience, heart and brain coherence refers to a state in which our heart and brain “vibrate” in synchrony. This means that our heart rate patterns align with our brain waves, generating emotional balance, mental clarity, and a greater ability to influence our environment. When we are stressed, anxious or distracted, the heart and brain function in disarray: heartbeats are irregular and brain waves chaotic. On the other hand, when we achieve coherence, a harmonious pattern is created that promotes physical and emotional health, creativity, and more effective decision-making.

Authors such as Neville Goddard, Joe Dispenza, Gregg Braden and Bruce Lipton agree that this state of coherence is not only biological, but also a bridge to manifest positive changes in our lives. Goddard emphasizes the power of conscious imagination: by visualizing with emotion and conviction, our cells receive signals that prepare the body for new realities. Dispenza supports this from neuroscience: when we align our thoughts and emotions, the brain creates new neural connections that reflect that internal coherence. Braden, for his part, demonstrates how the heart generates an electromagnetic field that can influence our environment and brain synchronization. And Lipton explains that our beliefs, by affecting cellular chemistry, can alter the way our bodies respond to the world; Therefore, a coherent heart and brain enhance positive biology.

In other words, everyone agrees that heart and brain coherence is a state in which our thoughts, emotions, and body work together as a synchronized team. This not only reduces stress, but amplifies our creativity, intuition, and ability to transform our reality.

Simple exercise to achieve coherence
    1. Find a quiet place: Sit comfortably and close your eyes.
    2. Breathe carefully: Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 1 second, exhale for 6 seconds. Repeat 5 times.
    3. Focus on the heart: Imagine that you breathe from the center of your chest, where your heart beats.
    4. Activate a positive emotion: Remember a moment of gratitude, love or joy. Feel that emotion deeply as you continue to breathe.
    5. Visualize your day or desired goal: With the emotion active, imagine your goal as if it were already happening. Maintain the feeling of fullness.

With just 3 to 5 minutes of this exercise a day, the heart and brain begin to synchronize, creating internal coherence that impacts your well-being, mental clarity, and ability to manifest what you want.
