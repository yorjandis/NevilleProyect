"autor": "Yorj",
"contenido": [
"Refuse to listen to unpleasant conversations, whether internal or external. Refuse to listen to anything destructive. Close your mind to any sensation that threatens your inner peace. Build a wonderful world in the depths of your mind where there is only room for love. Tibetan Buddhist monks have an expression of neutrality impervious to all disturbance: \"Amitabha,\" which means \"Infinite Light.\" In this way, only love can enter."
