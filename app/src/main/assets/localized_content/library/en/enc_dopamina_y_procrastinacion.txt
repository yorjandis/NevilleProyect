Dopamine and Procrastination

(why do you know what to do... and yet you don't do it)



🟢 What procrastination really is (at the brain level)

From neuroscience, procrastinating is:

Inability of the brain to anticipate a reward sufficient to initiate action.

Discipline does not fail.
Dopamine fails prior to action.



What happens in the brain

When you think about a task:
    • The brain evaluates effort vs. reward
    • If the reward is:
    • Far away
    • Abstract
    • Uncertain
→ anticipatory dopamine is not released

Result:
    • Feeling of rejection
    • Automatic search for easy stimuli (mobile phone, food, distraction)



🟢 The common mistake: trying to “motivate yourself”

Motivation does not precede action.

Neurobiologically:

Minimal action → dopamine → motivation → major action

Expecting motivation is expecting dopamine without action.
That doesn't happen.



🟢 Types of procrastination (according to dopamine)

🔸A. Procrastination due to distant reward

Example:
    • Study something whose benefit you will see in months

Solution:
    • Bring the reward closer in time



🔸B. Procrastination due to overload

Example:
    • “I have to do ALL of this”

The brain:
    • Cannot estimate reward
    • Lock startup

Solution:
    • Extreme fragmentation


🔸C. Procrastination due to previous hyperdopamine

Example:
    • Networks → try to work

Result:
    • Low basal dopamine
    • Nothing “stimulates enough”

Solution:
    • Eliminate peaks before working



🟢 The key mechanism: anticipatory dopamine

Dopamine is released before you act, not after.

But only if:
    • The step is clear
    • The effort is tolerable
    • The result is visible

If not:
    • The brain flees



🟢 Practical anti-procrastination protocol

🔸 Ridiculously small step rule

Define the first step so that:

Does not require motivation

Examples:
    • Open the file
    • Write a single line
    • Read a paragraph

🧠 This unlocks dopamine.



🔸 Design immediate micro-rewards

After the first step:
    • Short pause
    • Coffee
    • Music
    • Visual check ✔

It's not childish: it's applied neurobiology.



🔸 Eliminates previous dopamine

Before working:
    • ❌ Networks
    • ❌ Videos
    • ❌ Fast stimuli

Ideal:
    • Brief boredom (5–10 min)

This increases baseline motivation.



🔸 Fixed time, not fixed goal

Don't say:
    • “I'm going to finish this.”

Say:
    • “I work for 25 minutes”

The brain tolerates time, not ambiguity.



🔸 Rhythm before intensity

Better:
    • 30 min daily
What:
    • 5 hours once a week

Dopamine loves consistency.



🟢 The role of the body (very ignored)

Low functional dopamine appears with:
    • Lack of sleep
    • Thirst
    • Protein deficiency
    • Sedentary lifestyle

Practical solution:
    • Sleep well
    • Water
    • Protein in the morning
    • Previous movement



🟢 Signs that you are overcoming procrastination

✔ You start without thinking about it
✔ Less initial resistance
✔ Work “pulls” you
✔ You leave things half done (good sign)



🟢 Key phrase to reprogram your mind

I don't start when I feel motivated.
I feel motivated because I'm starting.



🟢 Daily mini-ritual (2 minutes)
    1. Choose a task
    2. Define the smallest possible step
    3. Do it without thinking
    4. Brand ✔
    5. Continue… or not (you already gained dopamine)



🟢 Final idea

Procrastination cannot be overcome with force,
You win by designing the correct start
