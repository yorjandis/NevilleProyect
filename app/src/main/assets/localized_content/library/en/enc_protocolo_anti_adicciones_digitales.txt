Anti-Digital Addiction Protocol (dopamine, habits and cognitive control)

Design of a serious, applicable and neurobiologically coherent anti-digital addiction protocol. It is not “leaving the cell phone”, it is reprogramming the dopaminergic system so that it responds to reality and effort again.

Think of it as detox + redesign + shielding.


CENTRAL PRINCIPLE

Don't fight willpower.
Change the environment and the stimulus-response sequence.

Digital addiction is not weakness: it is misdirected dopaminergic learning.



🟢 INITIAL DETOX (7–14 days)

Objective: recover dopaminergic sensitivity.

🔶1.1 Elimination of intermittent peaks

Delete (temporarily):
    • Social networks
    • Shorts / Reels / TikTok
    • News
    • Non-critical notifications

Neuroscience:
    • Intermittent dopamine is the most addictive
    • The brain needs silence to recalibrate



🔶1.2 Radical reduction of notifications

Rule:
    • Only real human calls and messages
    • Everything else → disabled

Practical example:

If it is not a person, it does not vibrate.


🔶1.3 Strict digital windows

Permitted use:
    • 2–3 blocks a day
    • Max. 20–30 min per block

Never:
    • Nothing upon waking up
    • Nothing before bed

This breaks automatic conditioning.



🟢 HABIT REPROGRAMMING (days 15–30)

Objective: make digital use conscious and functional.



🔶2.1 Eliminate “invisible triggers”

Physical changes:
    • Mobile phone outside the bedroom
    • Mobile away from the desktop
    • Black and white screen (very effective)

🧠 This reduces activation of the reward circuit.



🔶2.2 Effort → digital sequence

New rule:

Never consume content before a productive action.

Example:
    • Deep work → then messages
    • Training → then music
    • Reading → then content

This reverses dopaminergic learning.



🔶2.3 Substitution, not deletion

The brain needs stimulation.

Replace with:
    • Walks without headphones
    • Physical reading
    • Handwriting
    • Active learning

The void without alternative generates relapses.



🟢 PHASE 3 — LONG-TERM SHIELD

Objective: immunity against relapses.



🔶3.1 Design of the digital environment
    • Addictive apps → deleted
    • Clean browser
    • Minimalist desk
    • Friction login (long passwords)

Rule:

What costs, is used less.


🔶3.2 Diet information

Consume alone:
    • Long content
    • Deep content
    • Content with intent

Avoid:
    • Constant fragmentation
    • Multitasking
    • Passive consumption


🔶3.3 Daily ritual without artificial dopamine

Every day:
    • 30–60 min without stimuli
    • No music
    • No screens
    • Without objectives

This:
    • Restores receptors
    • Increase creativity
    • Reduces anxiety


🟢 SUMMARY DAILY PROTOCOL

Tomorrow
    • ❌ Mobile
    • ✅ Solar light + movement
    • ✅ Deep work

Noon
    • Short digital window
    • Physical activity

afternoon

night

    • ✅ Reading / reflection


🟢 SIGNS OF SUCCESSFUL RECONFIGURATION

✔ Fewer automatic impulses
✔ More tolerance for boredom
✔ Better concentration
✔ More enjoyment of effort
✔ Less need for stimuli



🟢 COMMON ERRORS (and why they fail)

❌ “Just a little”
→ Strengthens the circuit

❌ Willpower
→ Dopamine wins

❌ Multitasking
→ Fragments attention and motivation



🟢 KEY PHRASE TO REMEMBER

The cell phone is not the problem.
The problem is using dopamine without direction.
