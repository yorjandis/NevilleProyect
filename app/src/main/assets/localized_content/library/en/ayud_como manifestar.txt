Mentally construct a scenario in which your desire is fulfilled, and create a scenario that involves movement. Immobilize your physical body. Act precisely as if you were going to take a nap, and begin the predetermined action in your imagination.
A vivid reenactment of the action is the beginning of that action. Then, as you drift off to sleep, consciously imagine yourself within the scene. It doesn't matter what you sleep on; a short nap is sufficient, but carrying the action through to sleep materializes the fantasy into reality.

Source: Neville Goddard, Book: Imaginación Despierta
