Serotoin and Anxiety

Anxiety is not “overthinking,” it is primarily a poorly regulated neurochemical state, and serotonin is a centerpiece.


(why the mind doesn't shut up and how to restore stability)



🟢 What is serotonin (in relation to anxiety)

Serotonin is a regulatory neurotransmitter. Its main function is:

Point out security, stability and sufficiency.

When serotonin is adequate:
    • The nervous system interprets the environment as non-threatening
    • The mind can let go of excessive control

When it is low or poorly modulated:
    • The brain goes into hypervigilance
    • Increases anticipatory anxiety


🟢 Anxiety: what really happens in the brain

In anxiety, the following predominate:
    • Overactive amygdala (threat detection)
    • Fatigued prefrontal cortex (less control)
    • Low or dysfunctional serotonin
    • Elevated norepinephrine

Subjective result:

“Something is wrong, even if I don't know what.”


🟢 Types of anxiety according to the serotonin-dopamine axis

🔸A. Anxiety due to dopaminergic excess

Very common today.

It occurs when there is:
    • Lots of stimulation
    • Multitasking
    • Constant search for news

Symptoms:
    • Restlessness
    • Difficulty staying still
    • Need for distraction

Solution:
    • Raise serotonin
    • Lower stimulation


🔸B. Anxiety due to serotonin deficiency

Deeper.

It occurs when there is:
    • Baseline insecurity
    • Rumination
    • Circular thoughts

Solution:
    • Routine
    • Link
    • Regulate sleep and nutrition


🟢 Clear signs of low serotonin

✔ Difficulty relaxing
✔ Mind that does not turn off
✔ Irritability
✔ Need for control
✔ Vacuum after stimuli


🟢 How to increase serotonin naturally (the important thing)

🔸Rhythm and routine (key number one)

Serotonin loves:
    • Schedules
    • Repetition
    • Predictability

Application:
    • Sleep and eat at similar times
    • Simple daily rituals

This sends to the brain:

“Everything is under control.”


🔸 Smooth and regular movement

It's not HIIT here.

Better:
    • Walk
    • Yoga
    • Stretching
    • Calm swimming

Effect:
    • ↑ serotonin
    • ↓ norepinephrine

🔸Solar and nature exhibition

Sunlight:
    • Stimulates serotonin synthesis
    • Regulates circadian rhythms

Rule of thumb:
    • 20–30 min daily outdoors


🔸Real human bond

Safe social contact:
    • Raises serotonin
    • Reduces amygdala

Examples:
    • Conversations without a cell phone
    • Hugs
    • Presence without objective


🔸Slow breathing (direct tool)

Breathing:
    • Slow
    • Nasal
    • Longer exhalation

Example:
    • 4 s inhale / 6–8 s exhale

This:
    • Activates parasympathetic system
    • Promotes serotonin


🟢 Nutrition and serotonin

Serotonin is synthesized from tryptophan.

Useful foods:
    • Eggs
    • Turkey
    • Yogurt
    • Banana
    • Oats
    • Legumes

Facilitators:
    • Complex carbohydrates
    • Magnesium
    • Vitamin B6

Avoid:
    • Poorly managed fasts
    • Excessive stimulants
    • Alcohol


🟢 Common mistake: treating anxiety with more dopamine

Examples:
    • Networks
    • Ultra-processed food
    • Purchases
    • Excessive coffee

Momentary relief → greater anxiety later.

🟢 Key phrase to integrate

Anxiety does not ask for more control,
asks for a greater sense of security.

And that security is serotonin.


🟢 When to seek professional help

Important to say clearly:
    • Persistent anxiety
    • Panic attacks
    • Chronic insomnia
    • Functional impairment

Therapy and, in some cases, medication are not a failure, they are assisted neurobiological regulation.


🟢 Final idea
Serotonin allows you to inhabit the present without fear. Instead, dopamine pushes you towards the future.
