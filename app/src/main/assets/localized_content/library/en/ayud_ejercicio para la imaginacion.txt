Neville teaches us about the art of revision.

Here is a very healthy and productive exercise for your imagination. Something you should do daily. Relive the day each day as you wish you had lived it, revising the scenes to conform to your ideals. For example, suppose today's mail brought disappointing news; review the letter, mentally rewrite it, and make it according to the news you wish you had received. Write the letter to yourself and imagine that you received that letter.
