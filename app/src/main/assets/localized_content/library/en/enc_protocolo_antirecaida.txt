NEUROBIOLOGICAL ANTI-RElapse PROTOCOL

🟢 LAYER 0 · Preparation (before the impulse exists)

0.1 Precise habit mapping

Write (once):
    • Signal (time, place, emotion, body state)
    • Conduct
    • Real reward (not the theoretical one)

🧠 This activates prefrontal networks and reduces automatism.



0.2 Elimination of primary signals

Rule:

What you see, the brain executes.

Action:
    • Change the physical environment
    • Reduces accessibility
    • Increase steps

📌 This reduces anticipatory dopaminergic activation.



0.3 Design of the substitute habit

Must comply:
    • Same signal
    • Different response
    • Quick reward (calm, relief, body sensation)

Examples:
    • Stress → walk 5 min
    • Boredom → light reading
    • Anxiety → slow breathing

🧠 Compete directly in the striatum.



🟢 LAYER 1 · Early detection (when the impulse appears)

1.1 Internal warning signal

Train yourself to notice:
    • Body tension
    • Repetitive thinking
    • Physical urgency

Name:

“Impulse detected”

📌 Naming reduces automatic activity.



1.2 Mandatory physiological pause (90 seconds)

During that time:
    • Slow nasal breathing
    • Without deciding anything

🧠 Dopamine drops if the behavior is not executed.


🟢 LAYER 2 · Active diversion (before relapse)

2.1 Brief body movement

Example:
    • 20 squats
    • Walk 3 minutes
    • Stretching

🧠 The movement:
    • Reduces stress
    • Reactivates prefrontal cortex
    • Break the automatic loop



2.2 Immediate execution of the substitute

Without evaluating, without negotiating.

📌 The brain learns by action, not by intention.



🟢 LAYER 3 · Correct management of relapse (if it occurs)

3.1 Clean closure

After relapse:
    • Do not prolong
    • Do not repeat
    • Do not dramatize

🧠 You avoid reinforcing the circuit.


3.2 Post-event labeling

Mentally say:

“Old circuit activation”

This:
    • Reduces identification
    • Facilitates reconsolidation



3.3 Post-relapse soft cost

Within the next hour:
    • Walk 10 min
    • Cool shower
    • Short writing

📌 The brain associates habit → cost, without punishment.



🟢 LAYER 4 · Directed reconsolidation (hours – next day)

4.1 Technical review (not emotional)

Answer:
    • Which signal failed?
    • Body state?
    •    Around?

Change a single variable.



4.2 Dopaminergic micro-deception

The next day, at the signal:
    • Briefly remember that it was not that rewarding

🧠 Reduces future anticipatory dopamine.



🟢 LAYER 5 · System protection (indirect prevention)

5.1 Sleep

Less sleep = more relapses
It is physiology, not discipline.



5.2 Stable glucose

Hunger = striatum dominant.



5.3 Reduction of accumulated stress

Chronic stress blocks the change of habits.



🟢 LAYER 6 · Objective progress indicators

You are doing well if:
    • The impulse lasts less
    • You recover quickly after relapse
    • Behavior loses appeal
    • There is more space between signal and action

🧠 This indicates real weakening.



LAYER 7 · Golden rule (the most important)

The brain changes by tendency, not by perfection.

If you choose differently 70–80% of the time:
    • The old circuit loses strength
    • The new one is consolidated
    • Relapse becomes irrelevant



🟢 KEY CLOSING

You're not trying not to relapse.
You are teaching the brain that this path is no longer energetically rewarding.

When the brain really learns it,
the impulse ceases to have power.
