Dopamine vs Serotonin

(action vs well-being, impulse vs balance)



🟢 Fundamental difference

👉 Dopamine

Main function:
➡️ Drive towards something

It is associated with:
    • Desire
    • Motivation
    • Search
    • Progress
    • Anticipation

Brain question:

What's next?



👉 Serotonin

Main function:
➡️ Feel good about what is already there

It is associated with:
    • Calm
    • Satisfaction
    • Security
    • Emotional stability
    • Acceptance

Brain question:

Is it okay like this?



🟢 Common mistake (very expensive)

Modern society:
    • You have chronically high dopamine
    • Has low serotonin

Result:
    • Anxiety
    • Constant dissatisfaction
    • Inability to enjoy achievements

Typical example:

You achieve something important → you immediately look for the next thing.

That's dopamine without serotonin.


🟢 How they feel in the body and mind
🔸Energy: Dopamine (High) Serotonin (Stable)
🔸Attention: Dopamine (Focus on the Future) Serotonin (Present)
🔸Emotion: Dopamine (Excitement) Serotonin (Tranquility)
🔸Risk: Dopamine (Impulsivity) Serotonin (Prudence)
🔸Time: Dopamine("Later") Serotonin("Now")


🟢 When you need more dopamine

Clear signs:
    • Apathy
    • Lack of initiative
    • Procrastination
    • Feeling of stagnation

What to do:
    • Physical movement
    • Clear challenges
    • Small goals
    • Visible progress



🟢 When you need more serotonin

Clear signs:
    • Anxiety
    • Irritability
    • Emptiness after achievements
    • Constant need for stimulation

What to do:
    • Nature
    • Real social contact
    • Routines
    • Gratitude
    • Rest without objectives



🟢 How they regulate each other

🔁 Key relationship:
    • Dopamine rises → serotonin tends to fall
    • High serotonin → reduces dopaminergic urgency

Therefore:
    • A lot of ambition without rest → anxiety
    • A lot of calm without challenges → stagnation

Balance is dynamic, not fixed.



🟢 Daily practical applications

🔸Work
    • Tomorrow: dopamine (challenges, focus, creation)
    • Afternoon: serotonin (order, close, stabilize)



🔸Relationships
    • Dopamine → passion, novelty
    • Serotonin → bond, trust

Healthy relationships need both.



🔸Rest
    • Infinite Netflix → artificial dopamine
    • Calm walk → real serotonin

Serotonin

🟢 Dopamine, serotonin and happiness

Happiness is not dopaminergic.

It is:

Progress (dopamine) + satisfaction (serotonin)

Just dopamine:
    • Empty success

Serotonin only:
    • Peace without growth
