If I find it difficult to believe the truth of a state

(Taken from the book: Prayer: The Art of Believing, Chapter 5)

If the desired state is for yourself, and you find it difficult to accept as true what your senses deny, conjure up the image of a friend in your mind's eye and mentally have them affirm that you are already what you wish to be. This establishes in them, without their consent or conscious knowledge, the assumption that you are what they mentally affirmed, an assumption which, being unconsciously assumed, will persist until it fulfills its purpose. Its purpose is to awaken in you its corresponding vibration, a vibration which, when awakened in you, realizes itself as an objective fact.
