"titulo": "Doing vs Being",
"autor": "Yorj",
"contenido": [
    "The key lies not in DOING but in BEING. You don't have to do anything externally to bring about the manifestation your desire indicates; but you must always BE the desire before you can externalize it. Now I would like you to think about something very important: The manifestation of the desire is the final and unavoidable stage of your imagined act, but it should not be the culmination of your joy. Your joy must precede the manifestation itself. Don't let the shadows of what you call external reality steal the happiness you truly feel in your imagined act! It is your performance as a powerful child of God that you must love and for which you must seek happiness."
