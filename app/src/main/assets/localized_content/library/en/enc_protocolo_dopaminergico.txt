Neuroscience-based daily dopaminergic protocol designed to maximize sustained motivation, focus and habit formation, without burning out the system. It is not extreme or ascetic: it is strategic

MASTER PRINCIPLE:
🟢 Never put the reward before the effort.
First action → then stimulus.


🟢 Morning (07:00–10:00)

Goal: raise tonic (base) dopamine without artificial spikes.

🔶1.1 Wake up (first 60 minutes)

Absolutely avoid:
    • Mobile
    • Networks
    • News
    • Email

This prevents premature dopaminergic spikes that ruin your motivation for the day.

🔶1.2 Sunlight + movement (10–30 min)

What to do:
    • Go outside
    • Walk
    • Light mobility

Neuroscience:
    • Light regulates the dopamine-melatonin axis
    • Improves energy and focus throughout the day

Practical example:

Walk 15 minutes without headphones.

🔶1.3 Physiological activation (5–10 min)

Options:
    • Cold showers (30–90 s)
    • Intense breathing (light Wim Hof type)
    • Short bouts of exercise

Effect:
    • ↑ dopamine and norepinephrine
    • ↑ feeling of control
    

🔶 Deep cognitive work (90–120 min)

Here is the most important thing of the day.

Rules:
    • No dopaminergic music
    • No notifications
    • A single task

Example:
    • Schedule
    • Write
    • Solve complex problems

During sustained effort:
    • Dopamine increases gradually
    • Intrinsic motivation is reinforced

🔶 Mindful reward

Only after deep block:
    • Coffee ☕
    • Music
    • Short rest

This teaches the brain:

“Effort brings pleasure.”


🟢 Noon (12:00–16:00)

Objective: maintain dopaminergic stability.


🔶 Pro-dopamine nutrition

Food:
    • Sufficient protein
    • Healthy fats
    • Low sugar

Example:
    • Eggs + vegetables
    • Fish + legumes
    • Rice + protein + fiber

Avoid:
    • Heavy meals
    • Glycemic peaks
    

🔶 Main physical activity (30–60 min)

Ideal:
    • Strength training
    • Moderate cardio

Effects:
    • ↑ basal dopamine
    • ↑ dopaminergic receptors
    • ↓ anxiety

Rule:

Better to train before “rewarding yourself.”

🔶 Mechanical or social tasks

Good time to:
    • Meetings
    • Emails
    • Management

Dopamine is already stable, don't force deep focus here.


🟢 Afternoon (17:00–20:00)

Objective: avoid artificial peaks and preserve energy.



🔶 Controlled stimuli window

Here you can:
    • Networks (limited)
    • Recreational content
    • Music

Rule:
    • Maximum 30–45 min
    • With timer

This prevents:
    • Uncontrolled intermittent dopamine
    

🔶 Cognitive or creative micro-challenge

Examples:
    • Learn something new
    • Read something challenging
    • Personal project

This maintains:
    • Motivation without overstimulation
    

🟢 Night (20:00–23:00)

Objective: restore dopaminergic sensitivity.



🔶 Light and early dinner

Avoid:
    • Sugar
    • Alcohol
    • Intense screens

Prefer:
    • Light protein
    • Vegetables
    • Healthy fats
    
🔶 Conscious deceleration

Activities:
    • Calm walk
    • Stretching
    • Reading without screen
    • Diary

This lowers phasic dopamine and allows for a nighttime reset.

🔶 Sleep hygiene (key)
    • Sleep at the same time
    • Total darkness
    • Cool temperature

Sleep well:
    • Restores dopaminergic receptors
    • Improves motivation the next day
    

🟢 Golden rules (very important)

👉 Rule 1: Being bored is therapeutic

Boredom:
    • Restores dopaminergic sensitivity
    • Reactivate creativity



👉 Rule 2: Fewer options = more motivation

Eliminating unnecessary decisions conserves dopamine.



👉 Rule 3: Don't chase motivation, chase action

Action precedes dopamine, not the other way around.



🟢 6. Signs that the protocol works

✔ More initiative
✔ Less procrastination
✔ Enjoy the effort
✔ Less need for stimuli
