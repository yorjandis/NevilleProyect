Healthy and fulfilling habits, focused on body, mind and emotional well-being, with practical examples to incorporate them into daily life:

🟢 1. Adequate hydration
    • Drink enough water during the day (approx. 1.5–2 L, depending on your weight and activity).
    • Example: Always have a bottle of water on hand and set reminders every hour.

🟢 2. Sleep well
    • Maintain a regular schedule and sleep 7–9 hours.
    • Example: Turn off screens 30–60 minutes before bed.

🟢 3. Eat a nutritious breakfast
    • Include protein, fiber and healthy fats.
    • Example: Natural yogurt with fruits and nuts.

🟢 4. Eat more vegetables and fruits
    • At least 5 servings daily.
    • Example: Add vegetables to every meal, even in soups or smoothies.

🟢 5. Avoid ultra-processed foods
    • Minimize added sugars, refined flours and fast food.
    • Example: Prepare homemade snacks such as nuts or hummus with carrots.

🟢 6. Regular exercise
    • 150 minutes of moderate activity per week.
    • Example: Walking 30 minutes a day or strength training 3 times/week.

🟢 7. Conscious breathing
    • Deep and slow breathing to reduce stress.
    • Example: 5 minutes of 4-7-8 breathing before going to sleep.

🟢 8. Meditation or mindfulness
    • They reduce anxiety and improve focus.
    • Example: 10 minutes of guided meditation every morning.

🟢 9. Correct posture
    • Maintain spine and neck alignment.
    • Example: Adjust chair and screen when working.

🟢 10. Sunbathe moderately
    • Natural source of vitamin D.
    • Example: 15 minutes of sun on your arms and face in the morning.

🟢 11. Maintain social connections
    • Cultivate close and positive relationships.
    • Example: Call a friend or have dinner with family 2–3 times/week.

🟢 12. Learn something new
    • Stimulates the brain and reinforces plasticity.
    • Example: Read a book, learn a language or musical instrument.

🟢 13. Digital disconnection
    • Reduce time in front of unnecessary screens.
    • Example: Do not use your cell phone 1 hour before bed.

🟢 14. Daily gratitude
    • Promotes emotional well-being.
    • Example: Write down 3 things you are grateful for each night.

🟢 15. Planning and small goals
    • Maintain organization and motivation.
    • Example: Make a list of daily tasks with clear priorities.

🟢 16. Exposure to nature
    • Reduces stress and improves mood.
    • Example: Walking through a park, walking on trails or gardening.

🟢 17. Reduce caffeine/alcohol
    • Maintain optimal energy and sleep levels.
    • Example: Drink black coffee in the morning and limit alcohol to weekends.

🟢 18. Dental hygiene
    • Brushing, flossing and periodic check-ups.
    • Example: Brush twice a day and floss before bed.

🟢 19. Avoid smoking and drugs
    • Maintain lung and cardiovascular health.
    • Example: Replace habits with walks or creative activities.

🟢 20. Leisure and fun time
    • Vital for happiness.
    • Example: Listen to music, watch series or practice a creative hobby.

🟢 21. Daily self-reflection
    • Review emotions and actions.
    • Example: Journaling for 5–10 minutes.

🟢 22. Practice kindness and altruism
    • Improves emotional and social well-being.
    • Example: Help a neighbor or spend time volunteering.

🟢 23. Eat consciously
    • Avoid eating quickly or distractedly.
    • Example: Eat without a phone and chew slowly.

🟢 24. Reduce chronic stress
    • Identify factors and apply relaxation techniques.
    • Example: Yoga, meditation or relaxing baths.

🟢 25. Maintain healthy weight
    • Based on balanced diet and physical activity.
    • Example: Review progress and adjust diet and exercise.

🟢 26. Avoid constant multitasking
    • Improves concentration and quality of work.
    • Example: Work in blocks of 25–50 min (Pomodoro technique).

🟢 27. Listen to relaxing or inspiring music
    • Improves mood and creativity.
    • Example: Playlist of classical or instrumental music while you work.

🟢 28. Regular self-care
    • Spend time on yourself physically and mentally.
    • Example: Massages, skincare, relaxing baths or reading.

🟢 29. Laugh and enjoy moments
    • Promotes release of endorphins.
    • Example: Watching comedy, joking with friends or playing with pets.

🟢 30. Periodic medical check-ups
    • Prevention and early detection of problems.
    • Example: Annual checkup of blood pressure, cholesterol, vision and dentist.
