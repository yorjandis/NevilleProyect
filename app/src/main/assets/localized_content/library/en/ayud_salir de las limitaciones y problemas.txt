Breaking Free from Current Limitations and Problems
What you are currently facing in your world is the result of your previous imaginative activity. Your world is your materialized consciousness. Consciousness is what fills everything and is the cause of everything you see and experience in life. Scientists call it an energy field, the church calls it God, and esoteric schools call it the universal mind or divine energy. Phenomena such as electricity, magnetism, gravity, light, sound, thought, and feelings are merely manifestations or effects of this consciousness. Whatever name we give it, this consciousness or energy forms everything and possesses a special quality: It responds to impressions on the human mind. Upon registering an impression on consciousness, it rushes to create a visible mold in our world. In this way, everything that makes up our world is nothing more than the result of our internal activity.

Now, when we have understood this, then we know that:

1st: It is possible to transcend our current limitations. Second: An impression on the consciousness causes it to express itself in our world.

How do we do this? The truth is, it's very easy to achieve. All that's required is discipline and consistency. No physical effort or willpower is needed.

First, we must understand that like Lot's wife, who looked back and turned into a pillar of salt, retaining her former state of mind, we must REMOVE OUR ATTENTION FROM OUR LIMITATIONS AND PROBLEMS. That is, we must not think, remember, or imagine our limitations. Our attention is what perpetuates a manifestation. By removing our attention, we deprive it of its energy of expression. This causes it to gradually fade away.

Second, since the mind (consciousness) abhors emptiness, by removing our thoughts and attention from current limitations, we must instead place our attention on the thing or situation we wish to see in our lives. That is, we must discipline the mind so that from now on it only has thoughts about our desire. We must imagine our entire world with the new sensation of already possessing our desire.

Third, and most importantly, after we have withdrawn our attention from our current state and filled our mind with thoughts and feelings of our desired state, WE MUST FEEL AND EXPERIENCE THIS NEW STATE. We must feel within ourselves the naturalness of already having our desire. An example to illustrate this state is when we buy new shoes. At first, it feels unnatural and strange to wear them, but over time, they become an everyday part of our lives, natural, and we even forget to clean and polish them often. In the same way, when a desire begins to feel like a natural part of our lives because we experience it all the time, then consciousness begins to materialize it in our world. This is the key to its manifestation.

We must keep in mind certain things that could delay manifestation: We should never think about how the changes that the new state will bring will take place. That shouldn't concern us and is simply part of the hidden operation of the law of expression. We should just focus on enjoying and feeling the reality and naturalness of our desire.
