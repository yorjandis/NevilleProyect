Performing a Treatment on Another

A very effective way to bring new things to another is to conjure up in your mind's eye the subjective image of the person you wish to help and have them affirm that they have done what you wished them to do. Mentally hear them tell you that they have done it. This awakens in them the vibrational correlate of the affirmed state, a vibration that persists until its mission is accomplished. It doesn't matter what you wish to be done, or whom you selected to do it. As soon as you subjectively affirm that it is done, the results follow. Failure can only result if you fail to accept the truth of your assertion or if the state is not desired by the subject for themselves or for another. In the latter case, the state would manifest itself in you, the practitioner.

Source: The Book The Art of Believing, Chapter 5
