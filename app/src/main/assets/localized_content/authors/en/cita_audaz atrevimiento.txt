BOLD DARE - Lecture #64 - Neville 09/27/1968
Daring Boldly

There is no limit to the power of belief or the possibilities of prayer, but you must be boldly bold and not accept “No” for an answer. Try it!

When I say you're all imagination, I mean it.

As I stand here on the stand, I can, in a split second, imagine that I am outside, looking at this building. Or, in another second, being in London and seeing the world from there. You say it's all hallucination? That it's all in my imagination?
