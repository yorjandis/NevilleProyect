30 DAY PLAN – BIOLOGY OF BELIEF

This plan is practical and progressive, to put into practice the teachings of The Biology Of Belief. The approach combines awareness, reprogramming of the subconscious, stress reduction and creation of a favorable internal environment, without dogmas and with concrete actions.

🧠 GUIDING PRINCIPLE DURING THE 30 DAYS
I don't try to control my body, I cooperate with its intelligence.

🟢 WEEK 1 · BE AWARE (DAYS 1–7)

Objective: observe how your thoughts and perceptions influence your body.

🔸Day 1
Write for 10 minutes:
👉 “What do I believe about my health, my body and my abilities?”

🔸Day 2
Observe your automatic thoughts throughout the day.
Write down at least 5 recurring negative beliefs.

🔸Day 3
Identify physical signs associated with stress (tension, fatigue, digestion).
Relate them to mental/emotional situations.

🔸Day 4
Practice 10 minutes of slow breathing (4 seconds inhale / 6 seconds exhale).

🔸Day 5
Reduces avoidable stressful stimuli (news, networks, multitasking).

🔸Day 6
Write down 3 moments of the day when you felt calm and safe.

🔸Day 7
Weekly reflection:
👉 How does my body change when I change my mental state?



🟢 WEEK 2 · RESCHEDULE BELIEFS (DAYS 8–14)

Objective: begin to modify the subconscious.

🔸Day 8
Choose a key limiting belief (e.g. “my body is failing”).

🔸Day 9
Write it in positive and present:
👉 “My body knows how to self-regulate and adapt.”

🔸Day 10
Repeat that belief 3 times when you wake up and before going to sleep (calmly).

🔸Day 11
Visualize yourself for 5 minutes living from that new belief.

🔸Day 12
Detect when you act from the old belief. Just watch it.

🔸Day 13
Reinforce the new belief with consistent action (rest, real food, movement).

🔸Day 14
Reflection:
👉 What internal resistance do I notice? Where does it come from?



🟢 WEEK 3 · EXIT STRESS MODE (DAYS 15–21)

Objective: promote cell growth and repair.

🔸Day 15
Identify your top 3 sources of chronic stress.

🔸Day 16
Consciously eliminate or reduce one of them.

🔸Day 17
Walk for 20–30 minutes without a cell phone, just observing.

🔸Day 18
Before eating, take 3 deep breaths (activate repair mode).

🔸Day 19
Practice body gratitude: be grateful for 3 functions of your body.

🔸Day 20
Sleep is biology: prioritize going to bed 30–60 minutes earlier.

🔸Day 21
Body check:
👉 Where do I feel more relaxed than a week ago?

🟢 WEEK 4 · INTEGRATION AND COHERENCE (DAYS 22–30)

Objective: align mind, emotion and action.

🔸Day 22
It defines what it would be like to “live from biological trust.”

🔸Day 23
Align a daily decision with that vision (food, work, rest).

🔸Day 24
Reduce negative language (“I have to,” “I can't”).

🔸Day 25
Practice 10 minutes of conscious silence.

🔸Day 26
Observe your internal dialogue in difficult situations.

🔸Day 27
Consciously reframe an automatic reaction.

🔸Day 28
Write a short letter to your body acknowledging its intelligence.

🔸Day 29
Review physical, emotional and mental changes of the month.

🔸Day 30
Closure:
👉 What practices will I maintain as a lifestyle?
