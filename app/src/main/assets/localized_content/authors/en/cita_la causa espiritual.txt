THE SPIRITUAL CAUSE - Lecture #48 - Neville 05/03/1968
Cause

I urge everyone to think of time as valuable! Use each and every moment to plant a seed of thought that you want to experience.

So, when your thought-seed is harvested, remember the moment of sowing; Because every natural effect has a spiritual cause that occurs the moment you dare to assume that your desire is real.
