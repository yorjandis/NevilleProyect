Joseph Joe Dispenza (born March 22, 1962) is an American international speaker, author, and doctor of chiropractic, known for his teachings on the relationship between the mind, brain, biology, and personal change. His work is especially popular in the field of personal development.

Academic background:
• He began studying biochemistry at Rutgers University in New Jersey, although he did not complete this initial course of study.
• He earned a Doctor of Chiropractic degree from Life University in Atlanta, Georgia, graduating with honors.
• He completed postgraduate studies in fields including neurology, neurophysiology, cellular biology, and genetics.

A life-changing event:
A defining moment in his life came in 1986, when he suffered a serious accident while competing in a triathlon. He says that, instead of undergoing surgery, he recovered his mobility completely by practicing meditation and deep visualization techniques. This episode sparked his interest in the potential of the mind and brain to influence health and biology.

Professional career:
Since 1997, Dispenza has delivered lectures and workshops in numerous countries, combining elements of neuroscience, meditation, and personal transformation. His work has spread through seminars, international retreats, and online courses that explore how thoughts and emotions can influence the mind and body.
Alongside his work as a speaker, he has maintained a chiropractic clinic near Olympia, Washington, where he sees patients when he is not traveling internationally.

Published works:
Joe Dispenza is the author of several books, many of them international bestsellers translated into multiple languages. His work combines accessible explanations with practical exercises and meditations. His best-known titles include:

1. Evolve Your Brain: The Science of Changing Your Mind – Explores the foundations of neuroplasticity and how the brain can rewire itself through thought and intention.

2. Breaking the Habit of Being Yourself: How to Lose Your Mind and Create a New One – Focuses on breaking deeply rooted mental and emotional patterns in order to create a new identity and new behaviors.

3. You Are the Placebo: Making Your Mind Matter – Describes how beliefs and expectations can influence biology and health, using the placebo effect as its conceptual framework.

4. Becoming Supernatural: How Common People Are Doing the Uncommon – Expands on his earlier ideas by integrating concepts from quantum physics with extraordinary experiences of personal transformation.

He has also published complementary materials such as meditation guides, practical exercises, and digital programs designed to help people apply his methods in daily life.

The central idea of his work:
Dispenza’s work centers on the idea that the mind and emotions can directly influence the body’s biology, bringing together concepts such as:
• Neuroplasticity: the brain’s ability to reorganize its neural pathways.
• Epigenetics: how behavior and the environment can affect gene expression.
• Mindful meditation: used as a tool for reprogramming beliefs and mental patterns.

For Dispenza, changing repetitive thoughts and emotional states anchored in the past is the key to creating lasting changes in health, behavior, and personal life.

Current activity (2025):
Joe Dispenza currently continues his work as:
• An international speaker, leading workshops and seminars around the world focused on personal transformation and the application of science to well-being.
• An author and creator of online programs, with structured courses and live sessions for his followers.
• A proponent of science-based meditation, arguing that conscious practice can influence health and biology, based on research conducted by him and his collaborators.

His approach continues to attract a global audience interested in comprehensive personal transformation, while also prompting debate over the scientific validity of some of his claims.
