Quote from the Conference THE NEW CHRISTOLOGY 09/22/1967
So, in the Father's house there are many states of consciousness. Choose a state, enter it and occupy it. Your Father is the good shepherd and your desires are his sheep. When the good shepherd comes, he gathers all your desires for yourself and others and takes them to the field. How is this done? In your imagination! A friend's desire may not be your desire for him, but if it does not conflict with your ethical code and you are a good shepherd, you will represent your friend as telling you that he (or she) has it. In this way your sheep, after having heard your voice, will follow you to the fold. ~Neville Goddard
