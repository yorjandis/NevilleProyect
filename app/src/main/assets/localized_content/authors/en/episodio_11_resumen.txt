Episode 11 Summary: Space-time, time-space and creation

In this episode, Dispenza organizes his final conceptual framework. Distinguishes space-time (material, linear, predictable world) and time-space (domain of non-local potentials). This distinction seeks to explain why, according to his model, creating from the field is different from trying to change matter with matter.

In the space-time domain, identity is structured by body, environment, objects, places and personal history. From there, the person tends to experience separation (“me here, result there”). Dispenza affirms that this mode usually sustains lack and waiting.

To enter time-space, it proposes temporary disidentification of the known self: reducing reference to body/environment/time and operating from consciousness. In this state, his approach is that a possibility is not “produced” from scratch, but rather an existing possibility is tuned.

A key teaching of the chapter is functional assembly: coherent mind to select clearly, coherent heart to attract/sustain that connection. The formula, therefore, is not conceptual but operational and must be applied simultaneously.

It also reiterates a practical warning: if after the practice the person immediately returns to their old emotional state, they return to the energy of the past and the continuity of change decreases.

This chapter prepares the bridge to episode 12, where the complete application is presented in directed protocol format.

Precise technique extracted from the episode

Preparation to create from the field:

1. Reduce identification with what is known (body, environment, time).
2. Stabilize presence in broad consciousness.
3. Select a specific possibility with clear intention.
4. Activate heart coherence to sustain connection.
5. Maintain the state when returning to the daily routine.

Key practical principle

Without continuity of state after practice, the creation signal loses strength.
