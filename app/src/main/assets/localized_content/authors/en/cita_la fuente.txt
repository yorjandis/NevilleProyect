THE SOURCE - Lecture #69 - Neville 10/14/1968
Mountains

No matter what your desire is, remember: nothing is impossible. What is now proven was once only imagined, so start by imagining a state and convince yourself that you are in it.

Blake said: "The ancients believed that if you are self-convinced, it was so. There was a time with Imagination when a firm conviction moved mountains."

You can eliminate the seemingly mountainous obstacles that come your way simply by ignoring them and assuming the end. And if you have to go over the mountain, you will, or the mountain will be moved.

Whatever needs to be moved for you to fulfill what you have assumed will be done for you as long as you remain true to yourself, the source of all life.
