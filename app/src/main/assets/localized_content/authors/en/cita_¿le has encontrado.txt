Quote from the Conference HAVE YOU FOUND HIM? 09/15/1967
The other day I read this little statement from Sir James Jean, one of the greatest astrophysicists of all time. It said: “On this planet man cannot raise his hand and not disturb the most distant star.” This is where the drama is taking place. You cannot raise an arm, you cannot think, without affecting the most distant star. That's how great you are, because God became you so that you could become God. ~Neville Goddard
