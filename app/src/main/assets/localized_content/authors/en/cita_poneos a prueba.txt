Quote from the PUT US TO THE TEST Conference 09/25/1967
So, now you are urged to examine yourself to see if you are holding to the faith. Are you accepting the headlines you see in the newspaper as facts? That phone call you just received? The morning email or the TV news that suggests horrible, unpleasant things to you? A friend calls, and pouring out all the filth in the world tells you how bad things are and how they are destined to get worse. As you listen to him, his sorrows enter and are assumed by you. Now, if you understand this law that imagining creates reality, you should, like a computer, choose what you are going to allow in. And when the conversation is over and your friend's voice is still fresh and clear in your ear, listen to his modified words, the modified tone of his voice, and feel the joy emitted there. ~Neville Goddard
