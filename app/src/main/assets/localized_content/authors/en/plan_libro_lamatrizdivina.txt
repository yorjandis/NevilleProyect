Book THE DIVINE MATRIX

30-day daily plan to apply the ideas and concepts in the book.

Don't look for perfection, look for daily repetition. Change happens when you train your mind every day, even if it's just a little.

The exercises last 15–30 minutes, explaining what to do, how to do it and when to do it. This plan focuses on awareness, intention and emotional coherence, following the teachings of The Divine Matrix.



🟢 Week 1: Connection and presence

Objective: Recognize the interconnection between everything and begin to observe thoughts and emotions.

🔸Day 1 – Observe your interconnection
    • Tomorrow: Write down 5 ways your life is connected to other people, nature, or the universe.
    • Afternoon: For 5 minutes, observe your breathing and feel how your body connects with the environment.
    • Night: Reflect on how these connections affect your day.

🔸Day 2 – Thought Record
    • All day: Every time a thought generates strong emotion, write it down in a notebook: what you thought, what you felt, and how you reacted.
    • Night: Review and underline the patterns you notice.

🔸Day 3 – Awareness of emotions
    • Tomorrow: Make a list of emotions you want to observe today (joy, gratitude, curiosity).
    • During the day: Every time you feel an emotion, stop for 10 seconds, breathe and name it mentally.
    • Night: Write down how you felt most present during the day.

🔸Day 4 – Active gratitude
    • Morning: Before you get up, think about three things you are grateful for.
    • Afternoon: By meditating for 5 minutes, visualize each thing in detail and feel gratitude.
    • Night: Write down 3 additional things that happened today and be grateful for them.

🔸Day 5 – Presence in action
    • During routine tasks (such as cooking or walking): focus on every movement and sound, maintaining full attention.
    • Extra exercise: Take 3 deep breaths before starting any task.

🔸Day 6 – Observation of internal patterns
    • Tomorrow: Think about a recurring thought that causes you stress or fear.
    • During the day: Every time it appears, pause for 5 seconds and replace it with a positive thought.
    • Night: Write down what change you experienced in your emotional state.

🔸Day 7 – Weekly integration
    • Exercise: Review your notes from the week and write 5 learnings about your emotions, thoughts and connections with the environment.
    • Optional meditation: 10 minutes of conscious breathing focused on the heart.



🟢 Week 2: Intention and focus

Objective: Learn to focus the mind and emotion to influence reality.

🔸Day 8 – Define your clear intention
    • Write a specific intention (eg: improve relationships, health, project).
    • Read your intention 3 times out loud while feeling positive emotion (confidence, joy).

🔸Day 9 – Emotional visualization
    • Exercise: For 10 minutes, close your eyes and visualize your intention as if it had already been fulfilled.
    • Imagine sensory details: sounds, colors, smells and associated emotions.

🔸Day 10 – Body-emotion connection
    • Exercise: As you breathe, place your hand over your heart and feel how your body responds to the visualization.
    • Notice any physical sensations that reflect emotion (heat, vibration, relaxation).

🔸Day 11 – Reinforcing the intention
    • Morning and night: Read your intention, feel gratitude and deep emotion as if it were already reality.
    • During the day: Every time you think about your goal, accompany the thought with deep breathing.

🔸Day 12 – Aligned action
    • Identify a small action that reflects your intention (call, message, habit).
    • Do it consciously, with full attention and positive emotion.

🔸Day 13 – Coherence Record
    • During the day, review every thought and emotion. Ask yourself: “Is this aligned with my intention?”
    • Write down the findings and possible adjustments.

🔸Day 14 – Weekly integration
    • 10-minute meditation: Visualize your intention realized while feeling gratitude.
    • Reflect on small changes you noticed in your life.



🟢 Week 3: Emotion and coherence

Objective: Develop coherence between mind and heart.

🔸Day 15 – Recognize dominant emotions
    • During the day, notice which emotion dominates your experience.
    • Note if it is aligned with your intention or blocks it.

🔸Day 16 – Cultivate positive emotions
    • List 5 emotions you want to feel more (love, trust, gratitude).
    • Breathe deeply and spend 5 minutes feeling each emotion.

🔸Day 17 – Coherence technique
    • Breathe for 5 seconds inhaling, 5 seconds exhaling.
    • While breathing, visualize your goal and generate positive emotion. Repeat 5–10 minutes.

🔸Day 18 – Transform negative emotions
    • When fear, anxiety or frustration arises:
    1. Take 3 deep breaths.
    2. Name the emotion.
    3. Imagine replacing it with gratitude or trust.

🔸Day 19 – Guided coherence meditation
    • 10 minutes: visualize your goal while synchronizing breathing, heart and positive emotion.

🔸Day 20 – Action from positive emotion
    • Do something positive for someone else (help, kind message).
    • Maintain awareness of the positive emotion while you act.

🔸Day 21 – Weekly integration
    • Write down how your consistent emotions impacted your day and observed results.



🟢 Week 4: Manifestation and expansion

Objective: Consolidate the practice and experience the influence in reality.

🔸Day 22 – Observe the results
    • Write down any coincidences, opportunities or changes you have perceived since the beginning of the plan.

🔸Day 23 – Intention adjustment
    • Review your intention: adjust words or focus so that it is clear, positive and achievable.

🔸Day 24 – Connection with the future
    • Visualize your life a year from now as if everything had manifested.
    • Feel gratitude and joy for it.

🔸Day 25 – Synchronization with others
    • Share your intention with someone close and visualize positive results together.

🔸Day 26 – Integration meditation
    • 15 minutes of breathing and visualization feeling that your intention has already been fulfilled.

🔸Day 27 – Daily conscious action
    • Identify 3 actions today that are totally aligned with your intention and execute them with full awareness.

🔸Day 28 – Advance gratitude
    • Before going to sleep, be thankful as if you have already received what you want.
    • Feel gratitude in every cell of your body.

🔸Day 29 – Observation of the Matrix
    • Reflect on how your thoughts, emotions and actions affected your environment.
    • Note coincidences or synchronicities.

🔸Day 30 – Final integration
    • Summarize your learnings from the 30 days.
    • Create a short daily ritual (5–10 minutes) to continue cultivating intention, coherence, and gratitude.
