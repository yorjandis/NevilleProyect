THE CORE OF MAN - Lecture #18 - Neville 11/17/1967
wish

Take my word and test yourself. Take a phrase like my friend did, or an image that would imply the fulfillment of your wish.

Hold it in your mind's eye.

Do not tell it to anyone, but convince yourself of its truth and fall into a deep sleep (sleep).

If you do this, no power on earth can prevent your desire from being objectified (manifested).
