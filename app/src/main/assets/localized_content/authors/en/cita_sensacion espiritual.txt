SPIRITUAL SENSATION - Lecture #115 - Neville 05/16/1969
dare

Right now you can use your powerful imagination to assume that you are what your senses and reason deny at this moment.

Walk in this assumption, knowing that you are all imagination, and that all things are possible for you.

Dare to believe in the reality of your assumption and watch the world play its part in its fulfillment.

Your assumption may appear to be false when you first imagine it; but if you persist, it will solidify into fact, because God is the one making the assumption.

All the objective facts that you see here on earth are just shadows, which fade because imagination is their reality.
