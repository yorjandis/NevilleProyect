Book STOP BEING YOU

30-day daily plan to apply the ideas and concepts in the book.

Don't look for perfection, look for daily repetition. Change happens when you train your mind every day, even if it's just a little.

🟣 The result of this plan will be: The new you begins to be your default state.

🟣 The key is this: It is not about changing your life, but about changing your internal state... and life reorganizes itself

🟣 Base daily structure (every day)

30–40 minutes daily
    1. Meditation (15–20 min)
    2. Mental exercise / reflection (10–15 min)
    3. Mindful application during the day (5 min of intention)



🟢 WEEK 1 (Days 1–7): BECOMING AWARE OF THE “AUTOMATIC SELF”

Objective: detect thoughts, emotions and unconscious reactions.

🔸Day 1
    • Write:
    • Who am I today?
    • What emotions do I repeat daily?
    • Meditation: observe breathing and thoughts without intervening.

🔸Day 2
    • Identify 3 common emotions (e.g. stress, impatience).
    • During the day: every time they appear, mentally say “this is a pattern.”

🔸Day 3
    • Observe how your body reacts to those emotions (tension, acceleration).
    • Meditation: conscious body scan.

🔸Day 4
    • Detects repetitive thoughts (“the same thing always happens to me”, “I can't”).
    • Write them without judging.

🔸Day 5
    • Relate thoughts + emotions + situations.
    • Meditation: observe without reacting.

🔸Day 6
    • Practice mindful pauses before responding in everyday situations.
    • 3 deep breaths before acting.

🔸Day 7
    • Weekly review:
    • Which pattern is repeated the most?
    • What emotion defines my “current self”?

👉 Result: you are no longer the boss, you are observing him.



🟢 WEEK 2 (Days 8–14): UNINSTALL THE OLD ME

Objective: weaken automatic mental and emotional habits.

🔸Day 8
    • Choose ONE main pattern to change (e.g. anxiety).
    • Write it in detail.

🔸Day 9
    • Meditation: When that thought appears, don't follow it.
    • Just observe it and return to the breath.

🔸Day 10
    • Identify the emotion that fuels that pattern.
    • Key question:
Who would I be without this emotion?

🔸Day 11
    • During the day, break the routine (small conscious changes).
    • Example: walk differently, respond differently.

🔸Day 12
    • Meditation: visualize yourself acting differently in a real situation.
    • Don't imagine results, imagine behavior.

🔸Day 13
    • Practice staying in the physical present (senses, body).
    • Every time the mind goes to the past/future, it returns to the body.

🔸Day 14
    • Review:
    • When did I react less automatically?
    • Where did I feel the most conscious control?

👉 Result: the old self begins to lose strength.



🟢 WEEK 3 (Days 15–21): CREATE THE NEW SELF

Objective: install new thoughts + emotions.

🔸Day 15
    • Define your new self:
    • How do you think?
    • How do you feel?
    • How does it act?

🔸Day 16
    • Choose a high emotion (gratitude, calm, trust).
    • Meditation: generates that emotion for no external reason.

🔸Day 17
    • Combine thought + emotion:
    • “I am a serene person” + feel real serenity.

🔸Day 18
    • Visualize a difficult situation and act as the new you.
    • Feel the emotion before imagining the scene.

🔸Day 19
    • During the day, act “as if” you are already that person.
    • Small conscious decisions.

🔸Day 20
    • Deeper meditation:
    • Mental silence
    • Full attention in the internal space

🔸Day 21
    • Review:
    • What feels different in my body?
    • What reactions are no longer the same?

👉 Result: the new you starts to feel familiar.



🟢 WEEK 4 (Days 22–30): CONSOLIDATE AND LIVE FROM THE NEW SELF

Objective: stability and coherence.

🔸Day 22
    • Reinforces intention + emotion at the start of the day.
    • Key question:
How does my best version behave today?

🔸Day 23
    • See if old patterns return without punishing yourself.
    • Just return to the chosen emotional state.

🔸Day 24
    • Meditation focused on total presence.
    • Less viewing, more feeling.

🔸Day 25
    • Apply awareness in relationships:
    • Listen without reacting
    • Respond calmly

🔸Day 26
    • Write evidence of change (no matter how small).

🔸Day 27
    • Long meditation (25–30 min).
    • Remain in “not thinking.”

🔸Day 28
    • Live the entire day from the new you.
    • See how your experience changes.

🔸Day 29
    • Deep review:
    • Who were you 30 days ago?
    • Who am I now?

🔸Day 30
    • Defines:
    • What practices do you continue?
    • What emotion will be your daily basis
