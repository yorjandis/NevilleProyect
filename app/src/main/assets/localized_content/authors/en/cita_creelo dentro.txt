BELIEVE IT WITHIN - Lecture #132 - Neville 06/10/1969
believe

When I say that all things exist in the human imagination, I mean infinite states [of consciousness]; for everything that would be possible for you to experience now, exists in you as a state of which you are its operating power.

Only you can make a state come alive. You have to enter a state and animate it in order for it to materialize (manifest) in your world.

You can then go back to sleep and believe that the objective fact is more real than your subjective state into which you have entered; But I can tell you: all states exist in the imagination.

When a state is subjectively penetrated, it becomes objective in your vegetative world, where it will have many ups and downs and disappear; but its eternal form will remain forever, and can be revived and brought back into existence through the seed of contemplative thought.

So I tell you: the most creative thing in you is to enter a state and believe it to exist.



BELIEVE IT IN
