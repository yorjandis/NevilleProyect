Quote from the Conference THE CHALICE OF EXPERIENCE 10/27/1967
You are infinite love, but without the power of imagination, love itself is eternal death. Begin now to change your world to fit your acts of love, but you cannot do it without imagination. Start with yourself! Change your world and see that the power of God is within you. Then you will know what it is to drink the cup that the Father has given you. It was the infinite love of God that separated you and allowed you to fall, for this separation is a fall and yet the beginning of a new creation. Just as the seed falls from man and a new creation begins, you fell and a new creation began, for God came with you as your human imagination! ~Neville Goddard
