Book THE PLACEBO IS YOU

30-day daily plan to apply the ideas and concepts in the book.

Don't look for perfection, look for daily repetition. Change happens when you train your mind every day, even if it's just a little.

🟣 The key to this application method is: Change does not occur when you understand, but when you sustainably feel that you are already different. If at the end of the month you react differently to the same situation, the process is working

💡 Key tip:
    • Sustained emotion is more important than specific intensity.
    • Visualize and feel your intention as already realized.
    • Daily repetition generates lasting changes in your mind and body

🟢 WEEK 1 (DAYS 1–7): OBSERVATION AND SELF-AWARENESS

Objective: become aware of your automatic thoughts, emotions and habits.

🔸Day 1 – Conscious observer (15-20 min)

Exercise:
    1. Take 10 minutes to sit in silence.
    2. Observe your mind: mentally note the thoughts that appear.
    3. Without judging them, classify them as: worry, planning, remembering, criticizing.
    4. At night, write a brief record of your dominant thoughts and emotions of the day.

Tip: Don't try to eliminate them, just notice patterns.



🔸Day 2 – Identify limiting beliefs (15 min)

Exercise:
    1. Make a list of 3–5 phrases that say a lot and limit you (e.g. “I never have time”, “I can't change”).
    2. Ask yourself for each sentence: Is it absolute truth or a mental habit?
    3. Write a positive or neutral version of each sentence.

Practical example: “I can never concentrate” → “I can train my attention with daily practice.”



🔸Day 3 – Dominant emotion (15 min)
    1. During the day, every hour do a check-in: what emotion do I feel now?
    2. Write down how it affects your body (breathing, tension, energy).
    3. At the end of the day, identify the emotion that was repeated the most.

Goal: begin to notice that your mind directs your biology.



🔸Day 4 – Thought → emotion → body (15 min)
    1. When you notice an intense emotion, ask yourself: What thought triggered it?
    2. Write the sequence: thought → emotion → physical reaction.
    3. Reflect: is there a repeating pattern?

Example: Thought: “I'm going to fail the meeting” → fear → tension in the shoulders and rapid breathing.



🔸Day 5 – Basic meditation (10–15 min)
    1. Sit comfortably, back straight.
    2. Observe your breathing, feel how the air enters and leaves.
    3. When thoughts appear, return your attention to your breathing.

Goal: train the mind to observe without reacting.



🔸Day 6 – Body diary (15–20 min)
    1. Do a body scan: head, neck, shoulders, torso, legs.
    2. Notice any tension, heat, tingling or discomfort.
    3. Write how it relates to your emotions of the day.

Example: stress → clenched jaw → headache.



🔸Day 7 – Weekly integration (15–20 min)
    1. Review your diary for the week.
    2. Ask yourself: What patterns do I want to change first?
    3. Define a small goal for the next week.

⸻

🟢 WEEK 2 (DAYS 8–14): BREAKING AUTOMATIC HABITS

Goal: Interrupt automatic responses and start creating new ones.

🔸Day 8 – Declare intention (5 min)
    1. Write your intention clear and positive (e.g. “I live calmly and clearly”).
    2. Read it aloud 3 times.
    3. Close your eyes and imagine that it is already reality.



🔸Day 9 – Interrupt automatic reactions (full day)
    1. Every time you notice that you react impulsively:
    • Stop for 5 seconds
    • Breathe deeply
    • Ask yourself: Do I want to respond like this or from my new intention?

Goal: begin to separate stimulus → automatic response.



🔸Day 10 – Meditation + intention (15 min)
    1. 5 min: deep breathing and relaxation.
    2. 10 min: visualize your intention as if it were already reality.
    3. Add emotion: gratitude, joy or satisfaction.



🔸Day 11 – Conscious emotion (5–10 min)
    1. Choose a high emotion: gratitude, love, serenity.
    2. For 5–10 min, sit for no external reason.
    3. Observe how your body and energy change.

🔸Day 12 – Create your new “you” (10–15 min)
    1. Describe how your new self thinks, feels, and acts.
    2. Be specific: how do you respond to stress, to success, to criticism?



🔸Day 13 – Mental rehearsal (10 min)
    1. Visualize yourself facing real situations as your new self.
    2. Observe how your body and mind react differently.



🔸Day 14 – Weekly review (15 min)
    1. What ancient patterns appeared the least?
    2. Which ones still persist?
    3. Adjust your intention if necessary.

⸻

🟢 WEEK 3 (DAYS 15–21): CREATE NEW INTERNAL CHEMISTRY

Objective: train body and mind to sustain new emotions and thoughts.

🔸Day 15 – Mind-body meditation (20 min)
    1. Consciously relax each part of the body.
    2. Visualize your intention coming true.
    3. Maintain high emotion at all times.



🔸Day 16 – Sustained emotion (10 min)
    1. Choose a positive emotion.
    2. Maintain that emotion for 10 minutes concentrated.
    3. Notice changes in energy and breathing.



🔸Day 17 – Reprogramming before bed (10 min)
    1. Visualize what you want your next day to be like.
    2. Feel that it is already real.
    3. This helps your subconscious accept the new reality.



🔸Day 18 – Internal language (full day)
    1. Observe your internal dialogue.
    2. Each negative or limiting phrase: replace it with the positive version of your intention.



🔸Day 19 – Aligned action (full day)
    1. Identify an action that reflects your new identity.
    2. Do it consciously, as if you were already your new self.



🔸Day 20 – Deep meditation (25 min)
    1. Total silence, deep breathing.
    2. Visualization with intense emotion.
    3. Keep focus on your intention and new identity.



Day 21 – Internal evaluation (15 min)
    1. Observe if your reactions to external situations changed.
    2. Notice any differences in energy, calm, or confidence.



🟢 WEEK 4 (DAYS 22–30): CONSOLIDATE THE CHANGE

🔸Day 22 – New identity (10 min)
    1. Write sentences: “I am no longer…” / “Now I am…”
    2. Read them and feel the associated emotion.



🔸Day 23 – Conscious stress
    1. Each stressful situation: slow breathing, heightened emotion, conscious response.



Day 24 – Long meditation (30 min)
    1. Silence → intention → emotion → visualization.
    2. Maintain mind-body coherence.



🔸Day 25 – Real Aligned Action
    1. Do something you previously avoided, from your new identity.



🔸Day 26 – Advanced Gratitude (5–10 min)
    1. Be grateful for things that have not yet happened as if they were already real.



🔸Day 27 – Review of beliefs
    1. Identify beliefs that no longer have power over you.
    2. Write how you replaced them.



🔸Day 28 – Total coherence
    1. Maintain full attention and aligned emotions all day.



🔸Day 29 – Deep reflection (15 min)
    1. What changed in your behavior, emotions and energy?



🔸Day 30 – Final integration (20 min)
    1. Write a summary of your transformation.
    2. Decide what practices you will maintain in your daily life.
