BOOK: PURELY HUMAN

30-day daily plan to apply the ideas and concepts in the book.

Simple but profound 30-day daily plan designed to embody (not just understand) the teachings of Gregg Braden's Purely Human. The plan progresses from awareness → coherence → integration, with short practices (10–30 min/day) and fully applicable to real life.

🧩 Simple rules during the 30 days
    • Less perfection, more presence
    • Feel before analyzing
    • The body is an ally, not an obstacle

🟢 WEEK 1 · RECONNECT WITH THE BODY AND CONSCIENCE

Objective: get out of autopilot and regain presence.

🔸Day 1 – Conscious presence

Practice:
For 5 minutes, breathe slowly and observe your body without judging it.
Reflection: Where do you accumulate tension?
Key: the body is information, not an obstacle.

🔸Day 2 – Listen to the body

Practice:
Before eating, ask yourself: is this nourishing me or just distracting me?
Eat without screens.
Key: the body constantly communicates.

🔸Day 3 – Natural rhythm

Practice:
Slow down 10% in everything you do today.
Key: Coherence requires rhythm, not haste.

🔸Day 4 – Sensations and emotions

Practice:
Identify 3 emotions throughout the day and where they are felt in the body.
Key: emotion = biological signal.

🔸Day 5 – Conscious rest

Practice:
Before going to sleep, breathe for 5 minutes, bringing your attention to your chest.
Key: rest is regulation, not evasion.

🔸Day 6 – Silence

Practice:
10–15 minutes without stimuli (mobile phone, music, conversation).
Key: silence reactivates internal perception.

🔸Day 7 – Weekly integration

Practice:
Write: what has my body taught me this week?



🟢 WEEK 2 · HEART-MIND COHERENCE

Objective: align thought and emotion.

🔸Day 8 – Coherent breathing

Practice:
Breathe 5 seconds inhale / 5 exhale for 5 minutes.
Key: the heart orders the system.

🔸Day 9 – Heartfelt gratitude

Practice:
Choose something specific and feel it on your chest for 2 minutes.
Key: feel > think.

🔸Day 10 – Thought with emotion

Practice:
Visualize a desired result, feeling that it is already real.
Key: emotion precedes biology.

🔸Day 11 – Reaction vs response

Practice:
Before reacting, pause for 1 deep breath.
Key: consciousness = freedom.

🔸Day 12 – Internal language

Practice:
Notice how you talk to yourself today. Exchange criticism for understanding.
Key: biology listens.

🔸Day 13 – Conscious choice

Practice:
Choose an action aligned with well-being, not urgency.
Key: choosing is evolving.

🔸Day 14 – Weekly integration

Practice:
At what moments did you feel real coherence?



🟢 WEEK 3 · EMOTIONS AS TECHNOLOGY

Objective: use high emotions as a biological tool.

🔸Day 15 – Functional love

Practice:
Mentally send an emotion of appreciation to someone (without saying anything).
Key: the emotional field is transmitted.

🔸Day 16 – Conscious stress

Practice:
Detect a source of stress and breathe it in from your chest.
Key: stress is unintegrated information.

🔸Day 17 – Compassion with you

Practice:
Treat yourself today as you would treat someone you love.
Key: self-compassion = nervous regulation.

🔸Day 18 – Excitement before action

Practice:
Before an important task, generate calm or gratitude.
Key: The state determines the result.

🔸Day 19 – Body in coherence

Practice:
Walk for 10 minutes with slow breathing and attention to your chest.
Key: movement + coherence = integration.

🔸Day 20 – Presence in relationships

Practice:
Listen without interrupting or preparing responses.
Key: the connection is biological.

🔸Day 21 – Weekly integration

Practice:
What emotions clearly improve your energy?



🟢 WEEK 4 · LIVE PURELY HUMAN

Objective: integrate consciousness, technology and purpose.

🔸Day 22 – Conscious technology

Practice:
Use technology as a support, not as a substitute for perception.
Key: you decide, not the algorithm.

🔸Day 23 – Practical intuition

Practice:
Make a small decision guided by body sensation.
Key: intuition = integrated intelligence.

🔸Day 24 – Daily purpose

Practice:
Take an action aligned with meaning, not productivity.
Key: purpose regulates the system.

🔸Day 25 – Coherence in conflict

Practice:
When stressed, return to the chest before speaking.
Key: consistency defuses conflict.

🔸Day 26 – Natural creativity

Practice:
Do something creative without a goal (draw, write, think).
Key: creating is remembering who you are.

🔸Day 27 – Biological trust

Practice:
Trust your body today to set limits.
Key: the body knows before the mind.

🔸Day 28 – Conscious service

Practice:
Help someone from presence, not from obligation.
Key: giving in consistency strengthens.

🔸Day 29 – Integrated vision

Practice:
Visualize your life from heart-mind coherence.
Key: Sustained vision reorganizes biology.

🔸Day 30 – Closing and commitment

Practice:
Write:
What does it mean to me to live “purely human”?
What practices will you maintain?
