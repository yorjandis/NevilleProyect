PERCEPTION - Lecture #46 - Neville 04/26/1968
Attitude

When you change your attitude toward another, he must change his attitude toward you.

Were we not told, "We love him because he first loved us"?

Always start with yourself!

If you want him to be different, you must initiate the change. And by doing so, you will be practicing repentance, for the time is fulfilled and the kingdom of heaven is at hand.
