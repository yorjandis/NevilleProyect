Quote from the Conference THE SECRET OF PRAYER 06/10/1967
Prayer is a psychological movement. It is the art of moving from a problem to its solution. When a friend calls, telling me about a problem, we hang up, and I move from the state of the problem to its solution, hearing in my imagination the same woman telling me that the problem is already solved.

A friend recently shared this dream with me: We were in a garden and he told me all his wishes, when I said, “Don't wish for them, live them!” This is true. To wish is to think “in”! Living is thinking “from”! Don't go through life wishing. Live your desire. He believes it has already been fulfilled. Believe it is true; For an assumption, even if it is false, if persisted in, will solidify into fact. ~Neville Goddard
