Quote from the PROPHETIC SKETCHES Conference 09/22/1967
In the beginning you promised that you would assume the consequences of your imaginary actions, good, bad or indifferent. And you can from now until the end of time try to change the outside, but only when you change your way of thinking can you change your world. Give a man something on the outside to support him and you have conditioned his world and he will curse you when you stop doing it. But show him how to use his imagination to attract what he wants and you will have given him the gift of life. ~Neville Goddard
