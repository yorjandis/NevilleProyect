ETERNAL STATES - Lecture #60 - Neville 09/09/1968
States

Do you know what you want from life? You can be anything you want to be if you know who you are. It starts from the premise, “I am all imagination and pass through states,” for eternity (all things) exists now!

After you have experienced one state and moved to another you may believe that the previous state has ceased to exist, but all states are eternal, they remain forever.

As the mental traveler that you are, you pass through states whether consciously or unconsciously, but your individual identity is forever.

Whether you are rich or poor, you retain the same individual identity when you move from one state to another. If you are not on guard you may be persuaded by the press, television or radio to change your concept of yourself and unconsciously move into an undesirable state.

You can move to many states and play many roles, but like the actor, you do not change your identity.

When you are rich you are the same actor as when you are poor. It's just different roles you're playing.
