CONCEPCION - Lecture #34 - Neville 03/11/1968
Satisfaction

A goal, a desire, is an egg that you can penetrate and occupy. You can move directly inside and see the world from it.

Don't think about him; sees the world from it, which implies that you are in it.

Then you feel the relief, the satisfaction, of being there.

Do this and no power in the world can prevent you from realizing that state.

You may regret what you did, but you will do it anyway. Learn your lesson and try not to do what you don't want to experience in the future.
