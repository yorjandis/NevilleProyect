Quote from the Conference YOURS FOR THE TAKING 09/18/1967
Having been an alcoholic, this man imagined that his wife would never allude to her past; for although he had not touched alcohol for nine years, he had paid the price for his search for God. As you can see, the alcoholic is searching for the truth. Thirsty, he finds a false spirit in the form of alcohol, while those who do not try it – and criticize those who do – have not even begun their search. But I have news for them. One day they, too, will know a hunger that cannot be satisfied with bread. They will know a thirst so great that they will make the mistake of dressing it in the shape of a bottle. But because it will be a false thirst, the thirst will remain. Then you will discover true hunger and true thirst, which is to hear the word of God. ~Neville Goddard
