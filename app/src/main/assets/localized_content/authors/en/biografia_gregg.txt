Gregg Braden is an American author and educator whose work seeks to bring together science, spirituality, and human potential in order to offer new perspectives on life, health, consciousness, and humanity’s future. His career took him from a solid technical background in technology companies to becoming an internationally recognized voice through his books, lectures, and educational projects.

Gregg Braden was born in the state of Missouri, United States. From an early stage, he developed a technical background that led him to work in fields such as computer geology, systems science, and computer engineering.

From 1979 to 1991, Braden built his career in the technology and science sectors. He worked as a crisis problem-solver for Fortune 500 companies, holding significant technical roles, including Technical Operations Manager at Cisco Systems—a key company in the evolution of global computer networks—where he led technical support and operations teams during strategic periods in the company’s development.

Over time, this scientific and technical background merged with a deep curiosity about ancient traditions, ancestral cultures, and forgotten texts. This marked the beginning of his path as an independent researcher and educator exploring human potential in dialogue with modern scientific discoveries.

Published works—major books:
Gregg Braden has written numerous books that have been translated into more than forty languages, received awards, and appeared on bestseller lists. His most influential works include:

1. The Isaiah Effect (2000) – An early work, published by Random House, that explores the connection between ancient texts and contemporary science.
2. The God Code (2004) – Examines the possibility that ancient and genetic codes may reveal fundamental patterns of existence.
3. Secrets of the Lost Mode of Prayer (2005) – Explores forgotten spiritual practices with transformative potential.
4. The Divine Matrix (2007) – Discusses the idea of a “divine matrix” connecting every aspect of reality.
5. The Spontaneous Healing of Belief (2008) – Explores the relationship between beliefs, emotion, and healing.
6. Deep Truth (2010) – A journey through human origins, history, and destiny from an integrative perspective.
7. Fractal Time (2011) – Introduces the idea of fractal patterns in time as a way of understanding historical and future cycles.
8. The Turning Point / Resilience from the Heart (2014–2015) – A reflection on resilience, purpose, and global change.
9. Human by Design / The Science of Self-Empowerment (2018–2019) – Proposes understanding the human being as a system with natural capacities for self-regulation and evolution.
10. The Wisdom Codes (2020) – Words and meanings that Braden presents as tools for reprogramming the mind and heart.
11. Pure Human (2025) – A recent work reflecting on human identity, inner power, and the tensions between consciousness and technology in the future.

Beyond these titles, his books have inspired programs, courses, lectures, and documentaries around the world.

The central idea of his work:
Braden’s work is not limited to a traditional academic discipline. Instead, it proposes an interdisciplinary synthesis combining:
• Contemporary science, including physics, biology, and complex systems.
• Interpretations of ancestral texts and spiritual traditions.
• Observations on consciousness, emotional coherence, and human potential.

In many of his writings and lectures, Braden suggests that a “matrix” or deep field connects human beings with their surroundings, and that beliefs, emotions, and states of consciousness directly influence biology, health, society, and our collective future.

Themes he returns to frequently include:
• The relationship between thought, emotion, and perceived reality.
• The importance of heart–brain coherence for health and decision-making.
• Alternative interpretations of conventional scientific data from holistic perspectives.

His approach appeals both to people seeking to integrate science and spirituality and to those interested in personal growth and expanded consciousness. It also generates debate in strictly academic circles because it incorporates ideas that are not necessarily accepted by conventional science.

Current activity and international presence (2025):
Today, Gregg Braden remains active as an international speaker and educator. His work includes:
• International lecture tours and in-person workshops on science, consciousness, and human evolution.
• Educational programs and online seminars designed to bring his ideas into everyday life.
• Participation in global events and presentations for international organizations, companies, and groups seeking to transform our understanding of human potential.
• The publication of new books and multimedia content, with a particular focus on expanding consciousness and human coevolution in response to today’s technological and social challenges.

His work continues to inspire readers, philosophers, independent scientists, and spiritual practitioners interested in integrating scientific knowledge with profound human practices.
