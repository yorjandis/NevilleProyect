WALKING BY FAITH - Lecture #15 - Neville 11/06/1967
Spiritual Feeling

Ask yourself this simple question:

What would it be like if it were true that I am now the person I want to be?

Then you reach your feeling, your spiritual sensation. What's that? I will show you in a very simple way. Feel a piece of glass, now feel a baseball. Does the baseball feel the same as glass? Can you feel a tennis ball? Does it feel the same as a baseball or a piece of glass? Can you feel a piece of cloth, a violet, a piano? Do they all feel the same? Of course not. That is the spiritual sensation – a vivid way of seeing, hearing, smelling, tasting and feeling reality.
