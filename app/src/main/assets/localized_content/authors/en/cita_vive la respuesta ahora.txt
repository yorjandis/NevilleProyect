Wealth

Leave the state that contains poverty and move to the state that contains wealth and wealth will become reality. This room has reality and substance because you are thinking from it.

Think of a room, however, and it is nothing but a shadow. Think of a state and it seems like a mere possibility. But enter it thinking from the state and it is the only reality.

Blake said: “If the beholder could only enter the image in his imagination, if he could make a friend and companion of his image, he would rise from the grave and join the Lord in heaven.”

Now, buried in one state, you are a spectator of other states. But if you rose from your current state and buried yourself in another, you would express it. If you can be anything you want to be, why not become it?

Why settle in a state you dislike and argue, when you can move to another state in your imagination? But once you have moved to the state of your wish fulfilled, do not be like Lot's wife. Don't look back at your previous state and preserve it, because salt is a preservative.
