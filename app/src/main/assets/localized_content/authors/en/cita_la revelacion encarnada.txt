REVELATION INCARNATE - Lecture #89 - Neville 02/20/1969
I am

Any desire is yours to fulfill if you do not lose trust in the I AM. Add to [the I AM] anything and it will grow.

If your desire is to be rich, say to yourself: "I AM rich" – and think from that assumption.

If you want to be known, affirm that you already are. You can be anything you want to be through the act of assuming.

See your desire as if it were true now, and your assumption – although denied by your senses – when you persist in it will solidify into fact, becoming objectified and becoming a reality.
