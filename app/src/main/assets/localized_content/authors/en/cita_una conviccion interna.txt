AN INTERNAL CONVICTION - Lecture #35 - Neville 03/15/1968
Compliance

I tell you that imagination creates reality and I ask you to imagine a state, any state, that would involve the fulfillment of your desire.

It doesn't really matter what anyone else thinks; What you think is what matters to you!

If you create a scene involving the fulfillment of your wish and inhabit it until you have an inner conviction that it is real, what does it matter what anyone else thinks?
