Quote from the Conference A MOVEMENT WITHIN GOD 10/16/1967
Every conceivable situation you could ever think of exists now as a fact in God, but it cannot become visible to you until you occupy it, for you are the working power of God. Everything in this world needs man as an agent to express it. Hate or love, joy or sorrow, all things require man to express them. We glorify or condemn man, but he simply represents a state into which God knowingly or unknowingly entered and remained there until the state was externalized. Each one is free to choose the state he wishes to occupy. You imagined yourself in your present state. If you don't like it, you must imagine yourself outside of it and in another. It's all a question of movement. ~Neville Goddard
