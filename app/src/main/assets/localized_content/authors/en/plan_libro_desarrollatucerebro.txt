Book DEVELOP YOUR BRAIN

30-day daily plan to apply the ideas and concepts in the book.

Don't look for perfection, look for daily repetition. Change happens when you train your mind every day, even if it's just a little.

The method is based on the formula: Think + Feel + Repeat = New brain

🟣 BASE DAILY STRUCTURE (20–30 min)

This structure is maintained throughout the month:
    1. Morning (10–15 min)
Meditation + intention of the day
    2. During the day (5–10 min spread out)
Conscious observation of thoughts/emotions
    3. Night (5–10 min)
Reflection + visualization

⸻

🟢 WEEK 1 – BECOME AWARENESS (Get out of autopilot)

Objective: identify thoughts, emotions and automatic reactions.

🔸Day 1
    • Tomorrow:
    • 5 min slow breathing (inhale 4s, exhale 6s).
    • Intention: “Today I observe without judging.”
    • Day:
    • Every time you notice stress, ask yourself: What am I thinking now?
    • Night:
    • Write down 3 repetitive thoughts from the day.

🔸Day 2
    • Tomorrow:
    • Meditation: observe thoughts as if they were clouds.
    • Day:
    • Identify a dominant emotion (anxiety, hurry, calm).
    • Night:
    • Write down: thought → emotion → reaction.

🔸Day 3
    • Tomorrow:
    • Breathing + body scan.
    • Day:
    • Notice when your body reacts before your mind.
    • Night:
    • At what times did I act automatically?

🔸Day 4
    • Tomorrow:
    • Meditation focused on the heart.
    • Day:
    • Detects a recurring negative mental habit.
    • Night:
    • Write it down and give it a name (e.g. “the critic”).

🔸Day 5
    • Tomorrow:
    • Mentally repeat: “I can choose another answer.”
    • Day:
    • Conscious pause before reacting.
    • Night:
    • What changed when you paused?

🔸Day 6
    • Tomorrow:
    • Silent meditation (without guidance).
    • Day:
    • Observe how your state changes by breathing slowly.
    • Night:
    • List of emotional triggers.

🔸Day 7
    • Weekly review:
    • What patterns are repeated?
    • What emotion governs your day the most?

⸻

🟢 WEEK 2 – INTERRUPT AND RESCHEDULE

Objective: break patterns and choose new answers.

🔸Day 8
    • Tomorrow:
    • Visualize a difficult situation handled calmly.
    • Day:
    • When the old pattern appears, mentally say “stop.”
    • Night:
    • Write the new answer you tried.

🔸Day 9
    • Tomorrow:
    • Meditation + emotion of gratitude.
    • Day:
    • Consciously change a negative thought.
    • Night:
    • What new emotion appeared?

🔸Day 10
    • Tomorrow:
    • Visualize who you want to be in 6 months.
    • Day:
    • Act like that person once.
    • Night:
    • How did you feel?

🔸Day 11
    • Tomorrow:
    • Deep breathing + clear intention.
    • Day:
    • Observe how emotion directs your decisions.
    • Night:
    • What emotion do you want to train?

🔸Day 12
    • Tomorrow:
    • Meditation focused on confidence.
    • Day:
    • Repeat a new action even if it is uncomfortable.
    • Night:
    • Recognize the effort, not the result.

🔸Day 13
    • Tomorrow:
    • Visualize a complete ideal day.
    • Day:
    • Maintain coherence between thinking, feeling and acting.
    • Night:
    • Evaluate coherence of the day.

🔸Day 14
    • Review:
    • Which pattern lost strength?
    • What new response begins to be natural?

⸻

🟢 WEEK 3 – CREATE NEW NEURAL CONNECTIONS

Objective: consolidate new mental and emotional habits.

🔸Day 15-17
    • Tomorrow:
    • Meditation + visualization of the main objective.
    • Day:
    • Consciously repeat the new habit.
    • Night:
    • Mentally reinforces the achievement.

🔸Day 18–20
    • Focus on:
    • High emotion (calm, gratitude, security).
    • Practice holding that emotion for 2–3 minutes at a time.

🔸Day 21
    • Deep review:
    • What version of you is emerging?

⸻

🟢 WEEK 4 – NEW IDENTITY AND CONSISTENCY

Goal: Live like the person you are creating.

🔸Day 22–26
    • Tomorrow:
    • Declare: “Today I act like the person I want to be.”
    • Day:
    • Make decisions aligned with that identity.
    • Night:
    • Observe how your internal dialogue changes.

🔸Day 27–29
    • Refine:
    • Adjust visualizations and emotions.
    • Eliminate what no longer serves you.

🔸Day 30
    • Integration:
    • Write:
    • Who were you when you started?
    • What changed in the way you think, feel and act?
    • What practices will you maintain?
