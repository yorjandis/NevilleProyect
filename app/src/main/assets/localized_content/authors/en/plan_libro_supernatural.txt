SUPERNATURAL Book

30-day daily plan to apply the ideas and concepts in the book.

☘️ It's not about forcing results, but about becoming the person who already vibrates in that reality.

🟢 WEEK 1 — BE AWARE AND GET OFF AUTOPILOT

Objective: observe yourself and stop living in reaction.

🔸Day 1
    • Observe during the day what you think and feel most often.
    • Write at night: what emotions dominated my day?

🔸Day 2
    • Detects negative repetitive thoughts.
    • Don't try to change them, just consciously observe them.

🔸Day 3
    • Practice 10 minutes of slow, deep breathing.
    • Bring your attention to the body and notice tensions.

🔸Day 4
    • Identify a common emotion that limits you (stress, fear, guilt).
    • Ask yourself: what situation always activates this emotion?

🔸Day 5
    • During the day, every time you react automatically, pause for 5 seconds before responding.

🔸Day 6
    • 15-minute meditation focused only on observing thoughts without judging.

🔸Day 7
    • Written reflection:
    • What mental habits did I discover this week?
    • Where have I lived most in the past?

⸻

🟢 WEEK 2 — CREATE A NEW INTERNAL STATE

Objective: start changing your internal energy.

🔸Day 8
    • Choose how you want to feel more often (calm, confident, grateful).

🔸Day 9
    • 15–20 min meditation:
    • Remember a real moment where you felt that chosen emotion.
    • Relive it in the body.

🔸Day 10
    • Practice conscious gratitude for 5 real things in your life.

🔸Day 11
    • Observe during the day when you lose that elevated emotional state.
    • Don't criticize yourself, consciously return to it.

🔸Day 12
    • Meditation:
    • Bring attention to the heart
    • Breathe slowly
    • Feel gratitude for no specific reason

🔸Day 13
    • Reduces unnecessary stimuli (mobile phone, news, noise).
    • More silence = more awareness.

🔸Day 14
    • Written reflection:
    • What changes when I hold high emotions?
    • How does my body respond?

⸻

🟢 WEEK 3 — CREATE YOUR FUTURE FROM THE PRESENT

Objective: reprogram mind and body towards a new reality.

🔸Day 15
    • Define a future version of yourself (health, clarity, confidence, abundance).

🔸Day 16
    • Daily viewing (20 min):
    • Imagine that version acting, thinking and feeling.

🔸Day 17
    • Add emotion:
    • How does it feel to be that person already?

🔸Day 18
    • During the day, act like your “future self” once.

🔸Day 19
    • Meditation: feel gratitude for your future as if it already existed.

🔸Day 20
    • Observe mental resistance:
    • “This isn’t real,” “it won’t work.”
    • Let them pass without a fight.

🔸Day 21
    • Written reflection:
    • What part of me is changing?
    • What old habits are losing steam?

⸻

🟢 WEEK 4 — COHERENCE AND CONSOLIDATION

Objective: stabilize the change and make it natural.

🔸Day 22
    • Heart-mind coherence meditation (20–30 min).

🔸Day 23
    • Practice not reacting emotionally to a real challenge of the day.

🔸Day 24
    • Bring conscious attention to your posture, breathing and emotional tone.

🔸Day 25
    • Visualize how your body adapts biologically to your new state.

🔸Day 26
    • Live the day without complaining (either externally or internally).

🔸Day 27
    • Long meditation (30 min):
    • Silence
    • Presence
    • High emotion without images

🔸Day 28
    • Observe without judgment:
    • What internal changes are already evident?

🔸Day 29
    • Strengthen your future identity:
    • “This is who I am now”
    • “This is my new normal”

🔸Day 30
    • Final written reflection:
    • Who were you 30 days ago?
    • Who am I now?
    • What practices will I maintain?
