Book RESILIENCE FROM THE HEART

30-day daily plan to apply the ideas and concepts in the book.

This plan does not seek perfection, but presence.
Resilience from the heart is built every day, when you choose to respond from coherence and not from fear.

🟢 WEEK 1 — Reconnect with the heart (Days 1–7)

Objective: get out of mental autopilot and feel again.



🔸Day 1 – Become aware

Practice (10–15 min):
    • Sit quietly.
    • Bring attention to the chest.
    • Breathe slowly: 5 seconds inhale, 5 seconds exhale.

Written reflection:
    • How do I really feel right now?
    • What worries me most lately?

👉 Key: don't try to change anything. Just watch.



🔸Day 2 – Listen to the body

Practice:
    • During the day, notice when your body tenses.
    • Every time you detect it, take 3 deep breaths.

Practical example:
Before a meeting or decision, ask yourself:

“Does my body close or expand with this idea?”



🔸Day 3 – Identify automatic reactions

Practice:
    • Observe a strong emotional reaction of the day.
    • Note:
    • What happened
    • What did you feel?
    • How did you react?

👉 Objective: begin to differentiate stimulus from response.



🔸Day 4 – Create heart-mind coherence

Practice (5–10 min):
    1. Breathe slowly.
    2. Evokes an emotion of appreciation (gratitude, calm).
    3. Hold that feeling while thinking about a current challenge.

👉 This trains cardiac coherence.



🔸Day 5 – Ask the heart

Practice:
When faced with a small decision, ask yourself:
    • Which option gives me the most feeling of inner peace?
    • Which one causes contraction?

Don't rationalize. Feel first.



🔸Day 6 – Reduce mental noise

Practice:
    • Eliminate a source of overstimulation today (networking, news, multitasking).
    • Use that time to walk or breathe consciously.

👉 Resilience needs internal space.



🔸Day 7 – Weekly integration

Reflection:
    • What have I discovered about myself this week?
    • In what moments did I feel greater coherence?



🟢 WEEK 2 — Manage emotions and stress (Days 8–14)

Objective: stop reacting from fear.



🔸Day 8 – Naming emotions

Practice:
    • Give each emotion of the day an exact name:
    • Not “bad”, but “frustrated”, “anxious”, “insecure”.

👉 Naming reduces the emotional load.



🔸Day 9 – Breathing as an anchor

Practice:
    • 3 times a day:
    • 6 slow breaths from the chest.

Example: before responding to a tense message.



🔸Day 10 – Change the narrative

Written practice:
    • Write a current problem.
    • Then rewrite it as:
“This challenge is training me to…”



🔸Day 11 – Resilience ≠ endure

Reflection:
    • Where am I pushing myself instead of adapting?
    • What small adjustment would relieve tension?



🔸Day 12 – Conscious pause

Practice:
Before any emotional reaction:
    • Pause 5 seconds
    • Breathe
    • Choose to respond

👉 True resilience is born here.



🔸Day 13 – Active self-compassion

Practice:
    • Talk to yourself like you would talk to someone you love.
    • Key phrase:
“I'm doing the best I can with what I know now.”



🔸Day 14 – Emotional Review

Reflection:
    • What emotion appears most in me?
    • What message does it bring me?



🟢 WEEK 3 — Change, adaptation and purpose (Days 15–21)

Objective: use change as a lever for growth.



🔸Day 15 – Accept the change

Practice:
    • Identify a change you are resisting.
    • Write:
    • What do I lose?
    • What you could gain



🔸Day 16 – Conscious flexibility

Practice:
    • Voluntarily change a routine today.
    • Observe your emotions.

👉 You train your system not to collapse in the face of the new.



🔸Day 17 – Vision from the heart

Practice:
    • Imagine your life in 3 years if you act coherently.
    • How do you feel in that future?



🗓️ Day 18 – Daily micro-resilience

Practice:
    • Choose a small challenge.
    • Face it with presence, not haste.



🔸Day 19 – Learn from mistakes

Reflection:
    • A recent error:
    • What did it teach me?
    • How did it strengthen me?



🔸Day 20 – Daily purpose

Practice:
Before starting the day:

“How can I live a little more aligned today?”



🔸Day 21 – Weekly integration

Reflection:
    • How have I changed internally?
    • Where am I most flexible now?



🟢 WEEK 4 — Sustained Resilience and Relationships (Days 22–30)

Objective: consolidate the habit and expand it to your environment.



🔸Day 22 – Coherent relationships

Practice:
    • Listen to someone without interrupting.
    • Feel your chest while listening.



🔸Day 23 – Healthy limits

Reflection:
    • Where do I say “yes” when my heart says “no”?



🔸Day 24 – Community and support

Practice:
    • Share something authentic with someone you trust.



🔸Day 25 – Resilience in conflict

Practice:
    • In the event of a disagreement:
    • Breathe
    • Seek understanding, not winning



🔸Day 26 – Personal rituals

Practice:
    • Design a 5-minute daily ritual (breathing, gratitude).



🗓️ Day 27 – Simplify

Practice:
    • Eliminates an unnecessary obligation.

👉 Less load = more resilience.



🔸Day 28 – Gratitude from the heart

Practice:
    • 3 things you are grateful for emotionally, not mentally.



🔸Day 29 – Your personal manual

Write:
    • What helps you when you are under pressure
    • What deregulates you?
    • What brings you back to the center



🔸Day 30 – Closure and integration

Deep reflection:
    • Who am I now compared to day 1?
    • What practices will I maintain?
