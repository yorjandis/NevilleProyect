When a man prays successfully he believes that he is what he wanted to be.
If I can feel that I am what a few seconds ago I knew that I was nothing but that I wanted to be, then I no longer have the hunger to be. I am no longer thirsty because I feel satisfied in that state. Then something shrinks inside me, not physically but in feeling, in my consciousness, because that is the creativity of man. Thus your desire is reduced, the desire to continue in this meditation is lost. One does not physically stop, one simply has no desire to continue the meditative act.

'When you pray believe that you have received it and you will receive it.' When the physical creative act is completed, the tendon over the hollow of the man's thigh shrinks, and the man is helpless or prevented from continuing. In the same way, when a man prays successfully he believes that he already is what he desired to be, therefore he cannot continue to desire to be what he is already conscious of being. At the moment of satisfaction, physical and psychological, something is projected outwards and over time bears witness to the creative power of man.

Neville Goddard
