Quote from the Conference JUDAS THE REVEALER 02/10/1967
Test your own wonderful human imagination tonight and believe in the reality of Christ, your creative power. He believes that all things are possible for him. Imagine the state you want to express in this world, and as you go about your business you will see how quickly it will happen. Then one day the Scriptures will unfold in you, with you playing the central role, but in the meantime you are free to choose what you want to be or do. ~Neville Goddard
